Decks support

Create the presentation you want, save the design, and use it again. Decks is available in beta for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Requirements: iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, or macOS 15 or later on an Apple-silicon Mac. Join the TestFlight beta.

Install the Decks beta

TestFlight is Apple’s app for installing and updating beta apps. You do not need a beta version of iOS, iPadOS, or macOS to use Decks; your device just needs to meet the requirements above.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on the iPhone, iPad, or Mac where you want to use Decks.
  2. On that device, open the Decks public beta invitation and choose View in TestFlight.
  3. Accept the invitation if prompted, then choose Install for Decks.
  4. Open Decks and choose your photographs to get started. Return to TestFlight for new builds and beta feedback.

If the invitation opens in a browser after you install TestFlight, open the invitation again on the same device. If TestFlight reports that the beta is full or unavailable, email contact@brianrenshaw.app. See Apple’s TestFlight instructions for installation help.

Send feedback

Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight, or email contact@brianrenshaw.app. Include your device, operating system, Decks build number, and the steps that led to the issue. Share only photos and details you are comfortable sending.

Build and save your layout

On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, start on Home. Choose a destination, such as Post 4:5, Story, or Threads, then a layout and your photos or videos. Decks shows the pages right away with Layout, Frame, and Camera tools, so you can set photos per page, the background, the frame, and camera details with your own photography before you create the project. You can keep adjusting the design in the editor.

Smart layouts, such as Singles & Pairs, give photos shaped like the page a page each and put photos shaped across it on a shared page. Build Your Own, on Home, makes a smart layout with your own rules. Read the smart layout builder guide →

Save a finished design as a template from the editor’s project options or export controls. Templates lists Favorites, Smart Layouts, and Your Templates. Choose a saved design to edit it, duplicate it for a different look, or use it with another set of photos. On Mac, use File → Save as Template. The Templates library also lets you export and import template files.

Templates remember the design. Choose whether to include text wording when you save. Camera-detail variables resolve against the new photographs rather than copying another photo’s settings.

Editor controls and gestures

Layout, Frame, Photo, Text, Camera, Stamp, and Caption open their own controls. On iPhone and iPad, tap the active tool to hide its panel or bring it back. Drag the handle to adjust the space for the controls and canvas. Wide iPad windows use a separate inspector beside the photograph.

The page scope controls apply layout and frame changes to the selected page or all pages. Photo, Text, and Camera controls work with the selected content. Use the page controls to move through your project and arrange pages. Blue selection outlines are editing guides and do not appear in exports.

Customize canvas-margin swipes in Settings → Editor → Gestures. Previous, Next, and Arrange Pages remain available as visible controls. On Mac, use the inspector, menus, and keyboard commands; Command-K opens the Command Palette.

Text and camera details

Add text in the Text tool. Move and style it on the page, or insert details from the photograph. A logo can hold onto a photo’s corner with the Photo Corner buttons. Position fields are named Horizontal and Vertical.

Camera details are one card per photo. Placement puts the card Under, Above, or to the Side of the photo in the page margin, On Photo, or Free. Shows chooses which recorded fields appear, including camera, lens, shutter speed, aperture, and ISO. Style sets the type, ink, and background, and Save Style keeps the look for any page. Seven Camera Details Designs on Home, such as Gear Band and Wall Label, build a whole page around the details. My Gear supplies camera and lens details for photographs without that information, including scanned film photographs.

Date stamps

Select a photo and open Stamp to print the date, time, place, or a short note on it. Set All Lines chooses Film, Digital, Clean, or Typewriter, and each line has its own editor for style, color, size, opacity, and position. Decks uses the date the photo was taken; Custom Date changes only the stamp. Place names come from the photo’s saved location through Apple Maps, and you can save favorite places. Exported photos keep GPS location only if Include Location is on.

Fujifilm simulations and recipes

Decks reads supported Fujifilm MakerNotes from original JPEGs. If those notes remain in the file, the Camera tool can show its recorded film simulation and available recipe settings. Edited or converted copies may no longer contain that information; RAF, HEIC, and PNG files are not read by the Fujifilm JPEG decoder.

With camera details on, turn on Fujifilm Recipe to add the recorded film simulation and settings to the card as a Card, Line, Dials, or Film Box. Settings the photo does not record are listed separately and never guessed. Use Edit Photo Details to supply or override values, and save your own recipes for reuse. A recipe name is something you enter or apply from a saved recipe; Decks does not infer a named recipe from EXIF.

These features display information. They do not apply film-simulation processing to your photograph. Missing settings remain absent until you provide them.

Frames, backgrounds, and page sizes

Frame controls offer plain and film-style borders, shadows, and backgrounds. Choose white, black, ivory, a custom color, colors sampled from the photo, or a photo/video background. Add photos to a tiled layout to fill available spaces before adding more pages.

Choose a page shape for the destination you have in mind. For YouTube edits, save a template to keep your photographs consistent across videos, then export files for your video project.

Prepare photographs for Aura Frames

Decks creates finished image files that you upload through the Aura app. It does not connect to your Aura account. You can prepare a single photograph, a pair, or a composition with a background and border.

  1. Add your frame. In Settings → Destinations → Add a Photo Frame, select your Aura model and orientation. On Mac, click Done and Save Changes.
  2. Choose Smart Layout. Select your frame on Home, choose Smart Layout under Frame layouts, and import a mixed batch. For a landscape frame, vertical photos pair up and horizontal photos get individual pages. Upright frames reverse the arrangement.
  3. Choose the pairs and style. Open the Layout tool. Tap or click two photos to swap them, or drag one onto another. Pick White or Black for pairs and White Border, Black Border, or Fill for individual pages. Review the crop before exporting.
  4. Upload through Aura. Export the finished images to Photos or a folder, then add them through Aura. Each paired composition is one image, keeping the pair together.

If the image does not fit as expected, check the Decks page ratio, the frame’s orientation, and the crop in Aura. Keep your original photographs so you can adjust the composition later.

Captions and saved presets

Open Caption to write captions alongside your pages. Insert photo details while you write, then save frequently used wording as a caption preset. Variables update for each photo. Applying a preset replaces the caption; Undo restores it.

Copy a caption and paste it into Instagram, Threads, or another app. The Caption Sheet tracks copied captions. Caption wording saved inside a project or template travels with that item, and caption presets sync with your other settings through iCloud.

Apply templates from Apple Photos

  1. Save a template in Decks.
  2. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Photos & Shortcuts and add the Decks Templates shortcut.
  3. Select one or multiple still photos in Apple Photos. Open Share, choose Decks Templates, and choose your saved template.
  4. Allow the requested photo access. The shortcut applies the design and saves finished copies to Photos.

After setup, the usual template workflow runs without opening Decks. Keep the Photos input connected to Shortcut Input when editing the shortcut. This walkthrough covers still photographs. The Apply Template action returns finished images to the next shortcut action; follow it with Save to Photo Album or Share. Save Photos with Template also accepts videos and may continue processing in Decks.

Saved templates are also available through Decks actions in Shortcuts. You can use them in your own shortcuts, including ones you invoke with Siri or the Action button. Larger jobs or permissions may ask you to continue in the app. If a template is missing, reopen the shortcut and select an available saved template.

Receive photos from Ingest

In Ingest, select still photographs and choose Send to Decks. Receive them in Decks for Mac, or use the supported sharing options for iPhone and iPad. Decks offers a new project or an existing project as the destination. Incoming Photos holds deferred and supported iCloud handoffs until you are ready.

Your Ingest originals stay in place. Use an Ingest version that supports your chosen handoff. See the Ingest handoff guide for current options.

Projects, templates, and iCloud

Projects and templates are saved locally. iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. With the same Apple Account, it syncs projects, templates, their media, and settings such as destinations, favorites, favorite places, Fujifilm recipes, My Gear, caption presets, the last date stamp style, and the order of layouts on Home. Appearance stays separate on each device. Let media finish uploading or downloading before continuing on another device.

Settings shows storage and sync status, with Sync Now. Keep the original media downloaded when you need to work offline.

Export your pages

Use Export to review dimensions, quality, and pages, then save or share the results. Photos export as JPEG or PNG and video pages as MP4. You can also create a contact sheet. Decks prepares files; post or upload them in the destination app.

On iPhone and iPad, photo Size offers Standard (1080 pixels wide), High Resolution (2160 pixels wide), and Custom. Custom lets you enter Photo Width; the page shape determines the height. JPEG also offers quality choices. Review the dimensions before saving.

Embedded Metadata includes separate controls for Include Location and Include Contact Details. Both are excluded by default. These settings do not remove text or camera details you have visibly placed in the composition.

If a project disappears from the main library after export, check Archive. Archive Project After Export moves the project there after saving or sharing; turn it off in export controls if you want the project to stay in the main library.
