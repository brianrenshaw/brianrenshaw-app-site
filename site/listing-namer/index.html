For real estate photos · macOS

Rename photos. Set their order.

Make listing photos easier to find, sort, and upload. Group JPGs by room, choose their order, and export clear, numbered filenames for realtors to use on the MLS, listing websites, and beyond.

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · No account required

1.2.1

01 / Organize

Group photos by room.

Choose a room in the sidebar, select photos, and press G to find a group or create one. Drag photos between rooms or reorder rooms in the sidebar. The filenames follow your delivery order.

02 / Name

Make filenames consistent.

Group numbers set the listing photo order; photo numbers set the sequence within each group. Leading zeros keep filenames in order when sorted by name: 01, 02, … 10. Save naming presets and realtor templates for the next shoot.

03 / Deliver

Keep the JPGs intact.

Review the proposed filenames before exporting. Rename in place, or copy or move photos into numbered group folders in the current folder or a chosen destination. Photos are never re-encoded, and conflicting names are flagged before writing.

Stay with the photos

A workflow for your keyboard.

Press ⌘K to open a centered command palette. Jump to a room, assign photos, apply a preset, or choose Change Group Name to rename the selected photo’s group—for example, Garage to Outside Garage. Use G to group photos and ⌥⌘← / ⌥⌘→ to adjust their order without dragging.
