For real estate photographers · macOS

Give every listing photo its place.

Organize a shoot into rooms and spaces, put the photos in order, and export consistent filenames. Keep the original JPG quality.

Download for Mac ↗Getting started →

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · No account required

Version 1.0.0. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Download the DMG, then drag Listing Namer to Applications.

01 / Organize

Put the shoot in order.

Select photos, press G, and find a group or create one. Add to Front Exterior without creating another Front Exterior. Change group positions and let the numbering follow.

02 / Name

Make filenames consistent.

Use an address, room names, and numbering to give the delivery a clear sequence. Save naming presets and realtor templates for the next shoot.

03 / Deliver

Keep the JPGs intact.

Review the proposed filenames before exporting. Copy to a delivery folder or rename in place. Photos are never re-encoded, and conflicting names are flagged before writing.

Stay with the photos

A workflow for your keyboard.

Move between Contact Sheet, Preview, and Split views. Pinch to resize thumbnails. Use Command-click for selections with gaps, G to assign a group, and Shift-G to reorder groups.

Your jobs resume from this Mac’s private library. There are no sidecar job files to manage, and no account or cloud service is required.
