macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · No account required

1.0.2

01 / Organize

Group photos by room.

Select photos, press G, and find a group or create one. Add to Front Exterior without creating another Front Exterior. Change group positions and let the numbering follow.

02 / Name

Make filenames consistent.

Group numbers set the listing photo order; photo numbers set the sequence within each group. Leading zeros keep filenames in order when sorted by name: 01, 02, … 10. Save naming presets and realtor templates for the next shoot.

03 / Deliver

Keep the JPGs intact.

Review the proposed filenames before exporting. Copy to a delivery folder or rename in place. Photos are never re-encoded, and conflicting names are flagged before writing.
