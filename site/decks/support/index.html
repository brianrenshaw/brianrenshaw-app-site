Decks support

Decks is available in beta for iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later. Join the TestFlight beta to install it.

Send feedback

Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight, or email contact@brianrenshaw.app. Include your device, iOS version, Decks build number, and the steps that led to the issue. Add a screenshot if it helps; share only photos and details you are comfortable sending.

Start a project

Choose a start on Home, pick a page size and background, and select your photos. Adjust the frame, camera details, text, or caption in the editor. Save a design as a template to use it again.

Export your pages

Open Export to review the pages, then choose Save to Photos or Share. Decks does not publish to Instagram or Threads for you; post the exported pages in the app you choose.

Read the privacy policy for details about local storage and optional iCloud sync.
