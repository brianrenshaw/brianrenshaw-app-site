Decks support

Decks is available in beta for iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later. Join the TestFlight beta to install it.

Send feedback

Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight, or email contact@brianrenshaw.app. Include your device, iOS version, Decks build number, and the steps that led to the issue. Add a screenshot if it helps; share only photos and details you are comfortable sending.

Start with a template

Choose a layout from Templates on Home. Follow the steps to choose a page size, background, and frame, then select your photos or videos. You can change those choices in the editor.

Make your own template

Use Create Template in Saved to start from scratch. You can also save a design from the editor: open Project Options and choose Save as Template. Give it a name so you can find it again. Edit or duplicate a saved template when you want a different version.

Write and copy captions

Open Caption in the editor to write a caption for each page. Insert camera details while you write. Copy a caption, switch to Instagram or another app, and paste it into your post. Return to Decks to copy the next caption; the Caption Sheet keeps track of your progress.

Use a template with Apple Shortcuts

After you save a template, Decks lists it in Shortcuts as “Save photos with” followed by the template name. Run that action from Shortcuts, Siri, Spotlight, or the Action button. Choose your photos and save the framed results without opening Decks.

To run it from the Photos share sheet, open your template’s Photos Shortcut instructions in Decks:

  1. Get the Decks Templates shortcut using the link in those instructions, and add it to Shortcuts.
  2. Copy the shortcut name shown for your template.
  3. In Shortcuts, duplicate Decks Templates, paste that name as its title, and choose your saved template in its Template field.

Then select photos in Apple Photos, open the share sheet, and run the shortcut you set up.

Export your pages

Open Export to review the pages, then choose Save to Photos or Share. Decks does not publish to Instagram or Threads for you; post the exported pages in the app you choose.
