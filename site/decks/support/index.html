Decks support

Decks is available in beta for iPhone and iPad running iOS 18 or later. Join the TestFlight beta to install it.

Send feedback

Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight, or email contact@brianrenshaw.app. Include your device, iOS version, Decks build number, and the steps that led to the issue. Add a screenshot if it helps; share only photos and details you are comfortable sending.

Start with a template

Choose a layout from Templates on Home. Select your photos or videos, then choose a page size, background, and frame using your own photos in the preview. You can change those choices in the editor.

Editor controls and gestures

Layout, Frame, Photo, Text, and Camera open their own controls. Tap the active tool to hide its panel; tap it again to return. Drag the handle to resize the controls and canvas together. Blue dashed outlines mark selected content and never appear in exports.

Use This Page or All Pages in Layout and Frame to choose which pages change. Photo, Text, and Camera target the selected item. The page indicator has Previous, Next, and Arrange Pages.

By default, swipe left or right from the empty canvas margin to change pages, up to open Arrange Pages, and down to hide details. Customize all four in Settings → Editor → Gestures, or open Gestures from the editor’s page menu. System edge and editing gestures remain available.

Arrange camera details

Open the sidebar’s Arrange action to place the photo left, right, top, or bottom. The card keeps its formatting and grows into the available space. Top and bottom cards sit midway between the photo and page edge. Changing the photo or page shape updates the arrangement. Removing the last card gives a single photo its space back when no text or logo remains. Undo restores the whole change.

Choose a background

Frame → Background → Color offers White, Black, Cream, colors sampled from the current photo, and a custom picker. Replace Background is also available here. Sampled colors are calculated on your device.

Add photos to a tiled page

Choose the tile layout and photo count, then use the plus button to add photos. Decks fills available slots on the current page before creating more pages with that layout.

Learn the app

Settings includes Replay Introduction and How To. Keyboard editing keeps the active field above the keyboard and restores your panel height when you finish.

Make your own template

Use Create Template in Saved to start from scratch. You can also save a design from the editor: open Project Options and choose Save as Template. Give it a name so you can find it again. Edit or duplicate a saved template when you want a different version.

Write and copy captions

Open Caption in the editor to write a caption for each page. Insert camera details while you write. Copy a caption, switch to Instagram or another app, and paste it into your post. Return to Decks to copy the next caption; the Caption Sheet keeps track of your progress.

Use a template with Apple Shortcuts

After you save a template, Decks lists it in Shortcuts as “Save photos with” followed by the template name. Run that action from Shortcuts, Siri, Spotlight, or the Action button. Choose your photos and save the framed results without opening Decks.

For the Photos share sheet, open Settings → Photos & Shortcuts in Decks and add the Decks Templates shortcut. Select photos or videos in Apple Photos, share to Decks Templates, then choose your saved template. Results save to Photos.

Export your pages

Open Export to review the pages, then choose Save to Photos or Share. Decks does not publish to Instagram or Threads for you; post the exported pages in the app you choose.
