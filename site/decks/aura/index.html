For Aura frames

Make your photos fit your Aura frame.

Aura adds bars beside vertical photos, or zooms in until someone’s head is cut off. Decks lets you lay out your photos in the exact shape of your frame, then share them straight to Aura. They fill the screen the way you want.

The shape of the problem

Vertical photos and Aura frames don’t always get along.

Most Aura frames sit sideways, and most phone photos are taken upright. When the two meet, the frame has to choose. It either shows your photo small with empty space on both sides, or it zooms in to fill the screen and crops off whatever doesn’t fit.

You can go into the Aura app and fix the crop one photo at a time. Or the Carver might pair two of your vertical photos together, but it picks which two.

From your photos to your frame

Lay it out first. Then share to Aura.

  1. 01

    Choose your frame.

    In Settings → Destinations, add your Aura model and orientation. Choose it in Layout Builder so Decks matches the screen’s shape and size.

  2. 02

    Add your photos.

    Choose Smart Layout for a mixed batch. It pairs vertical photos and gives horizontal photos their own pages. Swap photos to choose the pairs and pick how each group looks.

  3. 03

    Share to Aura.

    When your pages are ready, tap Share and choose Aura. The photos upload to your frame in the background. You don’t even have to open the Aura app.

Decks doesn’t sign in to your Aura account. Aura shows up in the share sheet, so finished images go straight to your frame. You can also save them to Photos and add them in the Aura app.

  1. 01

    Choose your frame.

    Pick your Aura model in Layout Builder. Decks matches its shape and size, and Smart Layout appears first.

  2. 02

    Select your photos.

    Choose vertical and horizontal photos together. A mixed batch is fine.

  3. 03

    Review the pairs.

    Vertical photos go two to a page. Horizontal photos get their own pages. An unmatched vertical photo gets a soft background.

  4. 04

    Pick a photo to move.

    Tap a photo you want in a different pair. Here, it’s the espresso photo.

  5. 05

    Swap it.

    Tap the photo you want in its place. Now the two autumn photos share a page. You can also hold a photo and drag it onto another.

  6. 06

    Pick the look.

    Choose White or Black behind each pair. Horizontal photos start with Black Border. A black border blends into the frame’s own black edge, so the photo looks like part of the frame. A white border or soft background reads as a background behind the photo.

  7. 07

    Fine-tune in the editor.

    Create the project to open every page in the editor. Adjust a crop, the margins, or add text, like any Decks project.

  8. 08

    Share to Aura.

    Tap Export, then Share, and choose Aura. The photos upload to your frame in the background. You don’t even have to open the Aura app.

On your shelf, across the room

Small things that help on a frame.

  • People see a frame from across the room. If you add text or camera details, make them larger than you would for Instagram.
  • Most Aura frames, like Carver, Aspen, and Walden, use regular screens. Aura Ink is the exception: its color e-paper shows fewer colors than a regular screen, so solid backgrounds and simple borders look cleaner on it than blurred backgrounds.
  • Making a frame as a gift? Lay out the photos in Decks first, then preload them through Aura’s gift setup before you wrap it. Follow Aura’s gift instructions →

Questions

Why use Smart Layout instead of automatic pairing?

Smart Layout prepares the whole mixed batch before upload. You can choose the pairs, set a consistent look for individual photos, and review every page. Each pair exports as one image, so it stays together in Aura.

What if I have an odd number of vertical photos?

On a landscape frame, the unmatched vertical photo gets its own page with a soft background. On an upright frame, the same applies to an unmatched horizontal photo.

Does Smart Layout recognize people or choose matching subjects?

No. It groups photos by shape and starts in capture-date order when dates are available. Swap photos to decide who or what belongs together.

How do my photos get onto the frame?

Export your pages, tap Share, and choose Aura. Aura uploads them to your frame in the background, so you don’t need to open the Aura app. If you’d rather, choose Save to Photos and add them in the Aura app later.

Does Decks connect to my Aura?

No. Decks doesn’t sign in to your Aura account. It makes the images, and Aura’s option in the share sheet sends them to your frame.

Will Aura still crop my photo?

Decks makes the image the exact shape of your frame’s screen, so it should fill the screen without cropping. That’s why you match the page shape to your frame first.

Why does my frame put bars beside vertical photos?

It’s called pillarboxing. A tall photo on a wide screen has empty space on each side. Decks fills that space with a second photo, a soft background, or a crop you choose.

Does Decks change my original photos?

No. Your originals stay as they are. Decks saves a new copy.

Do I need to be a photographer?

No. If you have photos on your phone and an Aura on your shelf, Decks is for you.
