Contact Sheet or Proof Sheet?

Export the design, or make an overview of pages.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

A Contact Sheet is your design

  1. Choose Contact Sheet on Home to arrange multiple photos together on a page. Keep its film edges, switch to Plain, or choose None.
  2. Open Export to save the sheet as you designed it. You’ll see Your Contact Sheet; each sheet becomes an image. Removing the film border does not change this behavior.

A Proof Sheet is an export option

  1. For other projects, Export offers Pages or Proof Sheet. Choose Pages for the individual finished page designs.
  2. Choose Proof Sheet to make a compact overview of the project’s pages. Open Proof Sheet Details for its options.
  3. You do not need to make a proof sheet to export a Contact Sheet template. Its layout is already the sheet.

Mac

A Contact Sheet is your design

  1. Choose Contact Sheet on Home to arrange multiple photos together on a page. Keep its film edges, switch to Plain, or choose None.
  2. Choose File ▸ Export… to save the sheet as you designed it. Each sheet becomes an image. Removing the film border does not change this.

A Proof Sheet is an export option

  1. For any project, choose File ▸ Export Proof Sheet… to make a compact overview of its pages.
  2. Choose the Paper, Look, and Film, add a Title or Camera, and choose whether to Use Original Photos and show Filenames. Then click Export JPEG… or Export PDF….
  3. You do not need to make a proof sheet to export a Contact Sheet layout. Its layout is already the sheet.
