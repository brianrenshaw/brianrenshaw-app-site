Projects, Archive & Recently Deleted

Find projects and keep your library organized.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Layout Builder, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Find a project

  1. Home → Projects lists projects by last activity. Tap one to continue editing; returning Home saves your changes.
  2. Use the project filter in Projects to switch between All Projects, Archive, and Recently Deleted.
  3. Use a project’s actions to rename it, open its All Captions, or manage its place in the library. Pin projects you want to keep out of automatic cleanup.

Understand cleanup

  1. Exported projects can move to Archive automatically. Inactive projects archive after 7 days and move to Recently Deleted after 30 days; pinned projects stay put.
  2. Restore a project from Archive or Recently Deleted when you need it again. Deleted projects are permanently removed after 30 days by default; change the period in Settings, including Never. Original and exported photos are never deleted.
  3. Project cleanup does not delete your original photos or the images you exported to Photos.

Mac

Find a project

  1. Click Projects in the sidebar (⌘2). Switch between grid and list at the top, and use Search Projects to find one by name.
  2. Double-click a project, or select it and press Return, to continue editing.
  3. Control-click a project to Open in New Window, Duplicate, Keep Project, Archive, or Move to Recently Deleted.
  4. Incoming Photos, at the top of Projects, keeps photos sent from Ingest or Shortcuts until you choose a new or existing project.

Archive and restore

  1. Archived and Recently Deleted are in the sidebar’s Library section. Control-click a project there to Unarchive or Restore it.
  2. Decks on iPhone and iPad archives inactive projects after 7 days and moves them to Recently Deleted after 30; with iCloud sync, you’ll see those changes here. Keep Project keeps a project out of that cleanup.
  3. In Recently Deleted, Delete Permanently… removes a project right away.
  4. Permanently deleting a project also deletes Decks’ copies of its photos and videos, here, on your other devices and in iCloud, unless another project or template uses them. Your Photos library and exported images are untouched.

Free up space

  1. Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ Storage shows how much space Decks uses on this Mac.
  2. Free Up Space… removes local copies that are safe in iCloud. Opening or exporting a project downloads them again. Media from permanently deleted projects is removed automatically.
