Smart Layout for Aura frames

Prepare vertical and horizontal photos together for a digital frame, then choose which photos appear in each pair.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Choose your frame

  1. In Settings → Destinations, choose Add a Photo Frame. Select your Aura model and orientation.
  2. On Home, tap your frame at the top. Home shows Frame layouts sized for it: Smart Layout first, then Two Side by Side (Two Stacked on an upright frame), Soft Background, Full Screen, and Thin Border. More Layouts shows the rest; Fewer Layouts hides them again.
  3. Tap Smart Layout and select the photos you want to prepare together.

Review the automatic arrangement

  1. Decks shows the pages right away, with a strip of every page below.
  2. For a landscape frame, vertical photos are paired two to a page and horizontal photos get their own pages. An unmatched vertical photo gets a soft background. Square photos get their own pages.
  3. For an upright frame, horizontal photos are stacked in pairs and vertical photos get their own pages.
  4. Within each group, Decks starts in capture-date order when dates are available. This is an arrangement by photo shape, not face recognition or subject matching.

Choose the pairs and their look

  1. Tap Layout in the tools at the bottom. Under Vertical Pairs (Horizontal Pairs on an upright frame), tap two photos to swap them, or drag one onto another. You choose which photos belong together.
  2. Choose White or Black for the pair background. Under Horizontal Photos (Vertical Photos on an upright frame), choose White Border, Black Border, or Fill for the individual pages. A black border blends into the frame’s own black edge, so the photo looks like part of the frame; a white border or soft background reads as a background behind the photo. Fill crops the photo a little to cover the screen; check faces and edges.
  3. If your photos suit another frame layout better, Layout suggests it. Choose Use to switch, or Keep to stay with Smart Layout.
  4. Tap Create and review the crop on each page in the editor. You can continue editing the result.

Share to Aura

  1. In the editor, tap Export, then tap Share and choose Aura. The Aura app uploads the pages to your frame.
  2. To keep a copy first, tap Save to Photos, then add the images in the Aura app.
  3. A paired Decks page is one exported image, so the two photos stay together instead of being left to Aura’s automatic pairing. Decks does not sign in to your Aura account.
  4. Save a design you like as a template. To sort another mixed batch by photo shape, start Smart Layout again.

Smart layouts for other destinations

  1. For any other destination, Home’s Smart layouts row offers Singles & Pairs, Singles & Threes, and Singles & Fours. Photos shaped like the page get a page each; photos shaped across it share a page, two, three, or four at a time.
  2. Build Your Own, at the end of that row, makes a smart layout with your own rules. See Build your own smart layout.

Mac

Start a mixed batch

  1. Open Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ Destinations ▸ Add a Photo Frame…. Select your frame and orientation, click Done, then Save Changes.
  2. Open Home (⌘1) and choose the frame from the size menu. Frame layouts lists Smart Layout first. Click it, then choose your photos from Photos and click Continue, or click Choose Files….

Review, swap, and finish

  1. Open Layout in the panel on the right. It shows Vertical Pairs and Horizontal Photos for a landscape frame, or Horizontal Pairs and Vertical Photos for an upright frame. An unmatched photo in the pairing group uses a soft background.
  2. Click two photos to swap them, or drag one onto another. Choose White or Black for pairs and White Border, Black Border, or Fill for individual pages.
  3. Click Create Project, review the pages, and use File ▸ Export… to save images to Photos or a folder. Then add them in the Aura app. The same grouping and crop considerations apply on Mac.

Smart layouts for other destinations

  1. For other sizes, Home’s Smart layouts row offers Singles & Pairs, Singles & Threes, and Singles & Fours. Click Build Your Own to make your own rules.
