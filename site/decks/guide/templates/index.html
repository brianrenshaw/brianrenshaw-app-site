Decks help/Getting Started

Start from Home

Choose a destination and a layout, then your photos, and adjust the pages before you create the project.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Choose a destination and layout

  1. Open Home. On iPhone, Home is the first tab in the bar at the bottom, beside Templates and Projects. On iPad, it is in the sidebar.
  2. Tap a destination at the top of Home, such as IG Post, IG Post 4:5, IG Story, Threads, or Video. Each one shows its page shape beside the name; a photo frame shows its own name, such as Aura Walden. Tap … for every destination. Choosing one here does not change your saved default.
  3. Where the page can turn, choose Vertical or Horizontal below the destinations. Stories, TV, and square pages already have a fixed shape.
  4. Home lists Favorites, Smart layouts, Layouts, and Camera Details Designs. Each row scrolls sideways, and each card shows sample pages in the page’s own shape.
  5. Tap a layout, such as One Photo, Two Photos, Four Photos, Gallery Print, Panorama, Strip, or Contact Sheet, then choose your photos.

Adjust the pages before you create

  1. Decks shows your pages right away. The current page is large, a strip below shows every page, and the title shows the count, such as 5 photos · 3 pages.
  2. Tap a page in the strip to see it. Tap Change Photos to pick a different set.
  3. Use the tools at the bottom: Layout, Frame, and Camera. Each opens a panel over the pages; tap the tool again or Close to hide it. On a wide iPad, the panel sits beside the pages.
  4. Layout switches to another layout, with smart layouts first. It also holds Photos per Page for one-photo and smart layouts, Photo Arrangement (Side by Side or Stacked) for two-photo layouts, and Panels for a panorama.
  5. Frame sets the Border and Background, and Depth for Gallery Print. Camera turns on Camera Details and chooses which details show.
  6. With more than one photo, choose All Photos or This Photo (All Pages or This Page for layouts with several photos) to decide what your change applies to.
  7. Tap Create to open the project in the editor. You can change everything there.

Put photos on shared pages

  1. In Layout, Photos per Page offers One, 2, 3, or Grid of 4. With 2 or 3, choose Stacked or Side by Side. Photos shaped across the page share a page; photos shaped like the page keep a page each.
  2. Turn on Only if taken within 10 minutes to share a page only between photos taken close together.
  3. Under the pages, Split Page gives each photo on the current page its own page. Join with Next puts the current page’s photos with the next page’s. You can also drag a page in the strip onto another page to join them. A page holds up to four photos.

Your destinations

  1. Setup chooses which destinations appear at the top of Home. Skip Setup opens the full catalog with a 3:4 starting canvas.
  2. Settings → Destinations lets you add destinations and photo frames. Run Setup Again revisits your choices.
  3. Settings → Start Page chooses whether Decks opens to Home, Templates, or Projects.

Arrange your layouts

  1. Touch and hold a card in Layouts, then drag it to a new position, or choose Move Earlier or Move Later. Decks remembers that order on your other devices.
  2. Choose Favorite in the same menu to add a layout to Favorites at the top of Home. Remove Favorite takes it off.

Mac

Choose a destination and layout

  1. Click Home in the sidebar, or press ⌘1. Choose a size from the menu at the top, and Vertical or Horizontal beside it. Changing the size here does not change your saved default.
  2. Home lists Favorites, Smart layouts, Layouts, and Camera Details Designs. Click a layout, or Control-click it and choose Use This Layout.
  3. Choose your photos from your Photos library and click Continue, or click Choose Files… to pick files in Finder.

Adjust the pages before you create

  1. Decks shows the current page large, with Page 1 of 3 and arrows below it, and a strip of every page. The bar at the bottom shows the count, such as 5 photos · 3 pages.
  2. Choose Layout, Frame, or Camera at the top of the panel on the right. Layout changes the layout, Photos per Page, and Photo Arrangement. Frame sets the Border, Background, and Depth. Camera turns on Camera Details and chooses the details.
  3. With more than one photo, Frame and Camera offer All Photos or This Photo (All Pages or This Page) to decide what your change applies to.
  4. On shared pages, Split Page and Join with Next sit under the strip. Drag a page onto another to join them.
  5. Click Create Project to open the project in the editor, or Cancel to go back to Home.

Your destinations

  1. Setup narrows the size menu to the destinations you choose. Skip Setup shows every size instead.
  2. Use the size menu at the top of Home to try another size. Custom Size… makes any shape between 1:10 and 10:1.
  3. Edit Destinations… in the size menu opens Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ Destinations. Add destinations and photo frames, choose a default, or Run Setup Again…, then click Save Changes.

Two-photo arrangements

  1. For Two Photos, choose Side by Side or Stacked under Photo Arrangement in the Layout panel.
  2. In the editor, open Layout and turn Vertical Pair on or off to change it later.

Arrange your starting layouts

  1. Drag a card in Layouts to a new position, or Control-click it and choose Move Earlier or Move Later. Decks remembers that order on your other devices.
  2. Control-click a layout and choose Favorite to add it to Favorites at the top of Home.
