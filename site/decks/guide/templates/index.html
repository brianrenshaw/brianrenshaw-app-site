Decks help/Getting Started

Start with Layout Builder

Build a reusable design around your own photos.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Layout Builder, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Choose a starting layout

  1. On Home, open Layout Builder. Choose the destination and page orientation, then pick One Photo, Two Photos, a collage, Panorama, Strip, Contact Sheet, or another starting layout.
  2. Choose your photos. Swipe through their previews as you adjust the design.
  3. Choose a background and frame. Use This Page or All Pages to control which pages change.
  4. Add optional camera details, then tap Create Project. You can fine-tune everything in the editor.

Your destinations

  1. Setup narrows the size picker to the destinations you choose. Skip Setup opens the full catalog with a 3:4 starting canvas.
  2. Use the destination selector above Layout Builder to preview another size. This does not change your saved default.
  3. Choose Vertical or Horizontal below the destination to orient the whole page. Photo frames use the orientation you selected for that frame; Stories, TV, and square pages already have a fixed shape. Strip and Contact Sheet keep their Native Size.
  4. Settings → Destinations lets you add destinations and photo frames. Run Setup Again revisits your choices; skipping it restores the full catalog.

Two-photo arrangements

  1. After selecting photos, choose Side by Side or Stacked above the layout preview.
  2. Your chosen arrangement stays selected when you change page shape or go Back and Next. In the editor, open Layout to change it later.

Arrange your starting layouts

  1. Hold a layout card on Home, then drag it into your preferred position. Decks remembers that order.
  2. Tap the ⓘ beside Start for a quick reminder of the walkthrough options.

Mac

Choose a starting layout

  1. Click Layout Builder in the sidebar, or press ⌘1. Choose a size from the menu at the top, then click One Photo, Two Photos, Panorama, Strip, Contact Sheet, or another starting layout.
  2. Choose your photos from your Photos library and click Continue, or click Choose Files… to pick files in Finder.
  3. Choose a background and frame. With more than one photo, choose All Photos or This Photo to decide which photos change. Use the arrows under the preview to see each page.
  4. Turn on optional camera details in Finishing Touches, then click Create Project. You can fine-tune everything in the editor.

Your destinations

  1. Setup narrows the size menu to the destinations you choose. Skip Setup shows every size instead.
  2. Use the size menu at the top of Layout Builder to try another size. This does not change your saved default. Custom Size… makes any shape between 1:10 and 10:1.
  3. Choose Vertical or Horizontal beside the size menu to orient the whole page. Stories, TV, and square pages already have a fixed shape. Strip and Contact Sheet keep their Native Size.
  4. Edit Destinations… in the size menu opens Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ Destinations. Add destinations and photo frames, choose a default, or Run Setup Again…, then click Save Changes.

Two-photo arrangements

  1. For Two Photos, choose Side by Side or Stacked above the background choices in the walkthrough.
  2. Your arrangement stays selected when you go Back and Next. In the editor, open Layout and turn Vertical Pair on or off to change it later.

Arrange your starting layouts

  1. Drag a layout card in Layout Builder to a new position. Decks remembers that order on your other devices.
  2. Or Control-click a layout and choose Move Earlier or Move Later.
