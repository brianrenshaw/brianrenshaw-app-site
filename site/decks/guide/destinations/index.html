Instagram, Threads, frames & video

Choose a layout for the place your photos will go.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Instagram & Threads

  1. On Home, tap a destination at the top: Post 3:4 or Post 4:5 for an Instagram post, Story for Instagram stories, or Threads. Then pick a layout and select your photos.
  2. Keep a consistent background and frame across a series. Panorama can split a photograph into ordered carousel panels.
  3. Export to Photos, select the finished images in order in Instagram or Threads, and paste the caption you prepared in Decks.

Aura & other digital frames

  1. In Settings → Destinations, choose Add a Photo Frame. Select the model and orientation to add its size to Home.
  2. With the frame chosen on Home, Frame layouts lists Smart Layout first for a mixed batch: vertical photos pair up and horizontal photos get a page each. In the start screen’s Layout tool, you can swap pairs and pick each group’s look. Or pick one of four layouts that avoid empty bars: Two Side by Side, Soft Background, Full Screen, or Thin Border. An upright frame stacks two horizontal photos instead. If your photos suit another layout better, Layout suggests it. More Layouts, under the four, shows the rest.
  3. Export the finished images to Photos. Add them in the Aura app or your frame’s usual uploader, then review that app’s crop preview.

Photos in videos

  1. Choose Video for a 16:9 page, or match your editing project’s shape. Tap More (…) at the top of Home to see every destination. A background can give vertical photos room inside a wide video.
  2. Add a title, photo credit, or camera details in Decks. Check that the text will be readable when the image appears in the video.
  3. Export JPEG or PNG pages at a suitable width, then import them into your video editor. Set the duration and transitions there. A still image export is not a finished video.

Mac

Instagram & Threads

  1. On Home, choose Instagram post, Instagram stories, or Threads from the size menu. Then click a layout and choose your photos.
  2. Keep a consistent background and frame across a series. Panorama can split a photograph into ordered carousel panels.
  3. Export to Photos or a folder. In Instagram or Threads, select the finished images in order and paste the caption you copied from Caption.

Aura & other digital frames

  1. Choose Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ Destinations and click Add a Photo Frame…. Check your model and orientation, click Done, then click Save Changes to add its size to Home.
  2. With the frame chosen on Home, Frame layouts lists Smart Layout first for a mixed batch: vertical photos pair up and horizontal photos get a page each, and the Layout panel lets you swap pairs and pick each group’s look. Or pick one of four layouts that avoid empty bars: Two Side by Side, Soft Background, Full Screen, or Thin Border. An upright frame stacks two horizontal photos instead. If your photos suit another layout better, Layout suggests it. More Layouts shows the rest. Save the result as a template for future batches.
  3. Export the finished images to Photos or a folder. Add them in the Aura app or your frame’s usual uploader, then review that app’s crop preview.

Photos in videos

  1. Choose Video (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.) from the size menu for a 16:9 page, or Custom Size… to match your editing project’s shape. A background can give vertical photos room inside a wide video.
  2. Add a title, photo credit, or camera details in Decks. Check that the text will be readable when the image appears in the video.
  3. Export JPEG or PNG pages at a suitable Image Width, then import them into your video editor. Set the duration and transitions there. A still image export is not a finished video.
