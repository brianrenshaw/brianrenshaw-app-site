Camera details & Halide credits

Add camera details to a photo, choose where they sit, and style them.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Add camera details

  1. Before you create a project, open Camera in the start screen’s tools and turn on Camera Details. In the editor, open Camera and switch Camera Details on for the selected photo.
  2. Or start from a Camera Details Design on Home: Gear Band, Wall Label, Spec Sheet, Film Strip, Corner Mark, Spine, or Details List. Each is a finished page built around the camera and exposure.
  3. To restyle pages you already have, open Templates in the editor (Templates… in the page menu at the top on iPhone, or Templates in the toolbar on iPad). Under Designs, choose This Page or All Pages, then tap a design. Your photos and words stay; the page takes the design’s look.

Choose where the details sit

  1. Placement puts the details Under, Above, or to the Side of the photo (Left or Right), On Photo, or Free. Under, Above, and Side place the details in the page margin, and the photo moves to make room.
  2. Drag the details anywhere. Drop them in a margin to snap them there, or leave them where you drop them on the photo.
  3. If there isn’t room beside a small photo, the details sit on it, and the panel says why. Try a smaller Size. Details on a film border, a panorama, or a Freeform page also sit on the photo.
  4. Size sets how large the details are.

Choose what they show

  1. Shows lists the lines on the card. Switch a line on or off, or reorder the lines. More Details… opens the photo’s full list, where you can add or edit any detail.
  2. For Fujifilm photos, turn on Fujifilm Recipe to add the recorded film simulation and settings. Choose Card, Line, Dials, or Film Box for how they look. Settings the photo does not record are listed under Not Recorded on This Photo; tap Add to enter a value you know.
  3. If the photo also has a separate Fujifilm card from an earlier version, Move into Camera Details shows that recipe with the camera details, in their look and place.

Style and saved styles

  1. Open Style for Arrangement, Type, Ink, and Background. Type uses the design’s own fonts by Default; Fonts… lets you choose any family, with open-source families listed first. Ink is Auto, Light, or Dark.
  2. Background is the Look’s Own, None, or a Plate with its own color, opacity, and corners.
  3. On a 9:16 page, Keep Inside Stories Safe Area keeps the details clear of the areas Instagram draws over. New details on a Story page start with it on.
  4. Styles, at the top of the panel, lists your saved styles. Tap one to use it on the selected details. Save Style saves how these details look and where they sit, for any page.
  5. Details from earlier versions keep their own controls. Use the New Camera Details converts them to the current card.

Halide credits

  1. For a Shot with Halide credit, tap it to open its controls. Change its wording, adjust its spacing and width, or remove its background.
  2. The Halide logo and text move as a group, so you don’t need to line them up separately.

Remove details

  1. Switch Camera Details off, or choose Remove Details from the Camera panel’s actions.
  2. Removing the last camera card recenters a single photo when no text or logo remains. Undo restores both the card and its arrangement.

When details are missing

  1. Decks can only read the metadata included in the imported file. Screenshots and photos saved by other apps may be missing camera or exposure information.
  2. Try the original photo, or use Settings → My Gear & Places and Custom Variables for information you want to supply yourself.
  3. Save your Fujifilm recipes in Settings → Fujifilm Recipes, then apply one in Photo Details. A saved recipe supplies its name and settings even when automatic detection is unavailable. Applying a recipe adds information; it does not change the photo’s colors.

Mac

Add camera details

  1. Before you create a project, choose Camera at the top of the start screen’s panel and turn on Camera Details. In the editor, open Camera in the inspector and turn on Camera Details for the current photo.
  2. Use Apply To to place the same details on the Selected Photo, All Photos on Page, or one Shared Page card.
  3. To restyle pages with a Camera Details Design, Control-click a page in the page strip and choose Apply Design or Apply Design to All Pages. The Templates menu in the toolbar also lists the designs under Apply Design to This Page.

Placement, size, and style

  1. Placement puts the details Under, Above, or to the Side of the photo (Left or Right), On Photo, or Free. Under, Above, and Side place the details in the page margin.
  2. Drag the details on the canvas; drop them in a margin to snap them there. Arrow keys nudge them.
  3. Shows lists the lines on the card. Turn lines on or off, reorder them, or click More Details… to add or edit a detail.
  4. For Fujifilm photos, turn on Fujifilm Recipe and choose Card, Line, Dials, or Film Box. Settings the photo does not record are listed under Not Recorded on This Photo.
  5. Open Style for Arrangement, Type (Default or Fonts…, open-source families first), Ink, Background (Look’s Own, None, or Plate), and Keep Inside Stories Safe Area on 9:16 pages. Styles lists your saved styles; Save Style keeps the current look for any page.

Remove details

  1. Remove Card takes the details off the page. Undo (⌘Z) brings them back.

When details are missing

  1. Decks can only read the metadata included in the imported file. Screenshots and photos saved by other apps may be missing camera or exposure information.
  2. Try the original photo, or click ⓘ in the inspector. Under Edit Photo Details, choose a category and type the values, or choose Apply Saved Gear.
  3. Save cameras, lenses, film, and processing in Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ My Gear. Match EXIF Names uses your names for every photo with that EXIF wording.
  4. Save your Fujifilm recipes in Settings ▸ Recipes, then choose the Camera category in Photo Details and use Apply Film Recipe. Applying a recipe adds information; it does not change the photo’s colors.
