Fujifilm simulations & recipes

Show the settings behind your photo and reuse your recipes.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Show recorded simulation details

  1. Decks reads available Fujifilm MakerNotes from supported original JPEGs. Edited or shared copies may have lost this metadata.
  2. Import an original Fujifilm photo, open Camera in the editor, and switch Camera Details on. Turn on Fujifilm Recipe to add the recorded film simulation and settings to the card, then choose Card, Line, Dials, or Film Box.
  3. Settings the photo does not record are listed under Not Recorded on This Photo. Decks does not guess them. Tap Add to enter a value you know.
  4. Without camera details, Fujifilm Film Simulation adds a separate Fujifilm card. Open Details to choose the fields to include, then use Text Style to adjust their appearance. An older separate card offers Move into Camera Details.
  5. If Fujifilm controls are hidden, check Settings → Fujifilm → Fujifilm Details. Automatic shows them when a photo contains Fujifilm information.

Save and use a recipe

  1. Open Settings → Fujifilm Recipes and save your recipe with a name and the settings you want to remember.
  2. In the editor’s Fujifilm Details, choose Apply Saved Recipe…. Review the included information for that photo.
  3. Use in Caption inserts the recipe information into your posting text. Recipe details describe the photo; showing them does not apply a film simulation or change the photo’s colors or its original EXIF.
  4. Recipe names come from you. Decks cannot recover a photographer’s custom recipe name from the camera metadata.

Mac

Show recorded simulation details

  1. Decks reads available Fujifilm MakerNotes from supported original JPEGs. Edited or shared copies may have lost this metadata.
  2. Import an original Fujifilm photo, open Camera in the inspector, and turn on Camera Details. Turn on Fujifilm Recipe to add the recorded film simulation and settings, then choose Card, Line, Dials, or Film Box. Settings the photo does not record are listed under Not Recorded on This Photo.
  3. Without camera details, turn on Fujifilm Film Simulation for a separate Fujifilm card. Click Details to choose the fields to include, then use Text Style to adjust their appearance. An older separate card offers Move into Camera Details.
  4. If controls are hidden, check Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ General ▸ Fujifilm Details. Missing metadata can be entered through Details ▸ Edit Photo Details….

Save and use a recipe

  1. Open Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ Recipes, click Add, and give your recipe a name and the settings you want to remember. Click Save.
  2. Back in the editor, click Details beside the film simulation and choose Apply Saved Recipe…. Review the included information for that photo.
  3. Use in Caption inserts the recipe information into your posting text. Presets ▸ Save as Default Preset… remembers the chosen fields and text style for new Fujifilm cards.
  4. Recipe details describe the photo; showing them does not apply a film simulation or change the photo’s colors or its original EXIF.
  5. Recipe names come from you. Decks cannot recover a photographer’s custom recipe name from the camera metadata.
