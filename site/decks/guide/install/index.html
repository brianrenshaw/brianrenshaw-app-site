Decks help/Getting Started

Install Decks and get started

Download Decks from the App Store on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, then choose your photos.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Follow the workflow below on your iPhone. Use the Mac notes for desktop-specific controls.

iPad

The same workflow applies on iPad. Editing controls may appear beside the canvas in a wide window instead of below it.

Decks 1.0 is free on the App Store. One download covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Requirements: iOS or iPadOS 18 or later; macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).

  1. On the iPhone, iPad, or Mac where you want to use Decks, open Decks on the App Store.
  2. Choose Get, then confirm with your Apple Account.
  3. Open Decks. After you choose your destinations, a four-screen tour covers Decks, Smart Layouts, Camera Details, and Shortcuts.
  4. Choose a layout on Home, then choose your photos. Make your first composition.

Decks is free to use with five export batches each calendar month. Read about free exports and Decks Pro.

If you used the TestFlight beta

Install Decks from the App Store on the same device; it replaces the beta build. With iCloud sync on, your projects and templates stay available across your devices. Purchases made in TestFlight were free tests and do not carry over to the App Store version, so choose a plan again in Settings → Decks Pro if you want Pro.

Mac

Open Decks on the App Store on your Mac, choose Get, then open Decks from Applications or Launchpad. The Mac app requires macOS 15 or later and Apple silicon.
