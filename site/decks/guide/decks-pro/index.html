Free exports and Decks Pro

Understand the free allowance, the seven-day trial, and purchase restoration.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Home, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Five free export batches each month

  1. Use every editing tool and preview Pro features for free. Export five batches each calendar month at full quality with no watermark. Smart Layouts can use this allowance.
  2. One photograph or all pages in a carousel counts as one batch. Exporting again after edits counts as another batch. Sharing existing output and retrying the same batch do not count again. Saving an editable project does not use an export.

Choose Decks Pro

  1. Open Settings → Decks Pro to see the current price for monthly, annual, and lifetime access. Each option unlocks unlimited exports and exporting camera details, Fujifilm recipes, and Film date stamps.
  2. The optional seven-day trial starts when you choose it. It does not enroll you in a subscription and never charges automatically.
  3. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your Apple Account. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase; buying it does not cancel an existing subscription.

Restore a purchase

  1. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Settings → Decks Pro → Restore Purchases.
  2. Use subscription management in Decks Pro or your Apple Account settings to manage or cancel a subscription. If access does not return, contact support with your device and Decks version. Do not send payment details.

Purchases during the beta

  1. Purchases made in TestFlight are free tests. They do not charge you and do not carry over to the App Store version.
  2. The App Store release is coming soon. Check the price shown by Apple before confirming a purchase in the released app.
