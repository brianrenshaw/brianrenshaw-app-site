Apply templates from Photos

Use one template with one photograph or a batch.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Follow the workflow below on your iPhone. Use the Mac notes for desktop-specific controls.

iPad

The same workflow applies on iPad. Editing controls may appear beside the canvas in a wide window instead of below it.

  1. Save a template in Decks.
  2. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Photos & Shortcuts and add the Decks Templates shortcut.
  3. Select one or multiple still photos in Apple Photos. Open Share, choose Decks Templates, and choose your saved template.
  4. Allow the requested photo access. The shortcut applies the design and saves finished copies to Photos.

After setup, the usual template workflow runs without opening Decks. Keep the Photos input connected to Shortcut Input when editing the shortcut. This walkthrough covers still photographs. The Apply Template action returns finished images to the next shortcut action; follow it with Save to Photo Album or Share. Save Photos with Template also accepts videos and may continue processing in Decks.

Saved templates are also available through Decks actions in Shortcuts. You can use them in your own shortcuts, including ones you invoke with Siri or the Action button. More than eight photos, video, or a Photos permission request may bring Decks forward. If a template is missing, reopen the shortcut and select an available saved template.

Mac

Use a template from Photos

  1. Save a reusable template first. Then, in the Shortcuts app, add Select Photos, Decks’ Apply Template, and Save to Photo Album or Save File.
  2. Apply Template returns finished copies without adding an editable project. Save Photos with Template saves straight to Photos. Use Create Project from Photos when you want to edit the result in Decks.
  3. Before a shortcut saves to Photos, click Allow Saving to Photos in Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ General.
  4. For Terminal, AppleScript, and folder access, see Automate on your Mac.
