Decks help/Getting Started

Start with your photos

Skip the walkthrough and build as you go.

iPhoneiPadMac

iPhone

Start with the steps below. Editing tools appear beneath the canvas; tap the active tool to hide or show its controls.

iPad

Use the same task steps. The sidebar gives you access to Layout Builder, Templates, and Projects. In a wide editor window, controls can sit alongside the canvas; narrower windows use the compact panel. A missing control may be inside a collapsed section or the page’s options.

Add photos or videos

  1. Tap Add Photos & Videos at the bottom of Home. It’s available in Layout Builder, Templates, and Projects.
  2. Select your photos or clips, then confirm your selection. Decks opens a project using your New Project Defaults.
  3. Open Layout to choose the page shape and arrangement. Open Frame to choose None if you want a borderless start, then add styling when you need it.

Choose your defaults

  1. In Settings → New Project Defaults, choose the template and layout used for this route and for Photos → Share → Decks.
  2. Editing a project does not change those defaults. You can skip the walkthrough without losing the look you prefer.

Add more later

  1. Use Add Photos in the editor to bring in more images. New photos follow the project’s layout and fill available photo slots before adding pages.
  2. Videos come from clips you choose to import. Adding a still photo does not turn it into a moving video.

Mac

Add photos or videos

  1. Choose File ▸ Import Photos and Videos… (⌘O), drag files from Finder onto the Decks window, or paste them with ⌘V.
  2. To use your Photos library, click Photos in the sidebar, select photos or clips, and click the Add button. Decks opens a project using your New Project Template.
  3. Open Layout in the inspector to choose the page size and arrangement. Open Frame and choose None if you want a borderless start, then add styling when you need it.
  4. File ▸ New Blank Project starts with an empty page instead.

Choose your defaults

  1. In Decks ▸ Settings… ▸ General, choose the New Project Template used for imports and pasted photos. You can also Control-click a template and choose Set as Default.
  2. Editing a project does not change that default. You can skip the walkthrough without losing the look you prefer.

Add more later

  1. With a project open, choose File ▸ Add Photos and Videos… (⇧⌘O) or Add from Photos Library…, or drag files onto the editor. Each new photo gets its own page at the end.
  2. To lay out new photos like a saved design, choose File ▸ Add Pages from Template… instead.
  3. Videos come from clips you choose to import. Adding a still photo does not turn it into a moving video.
