Privacy Policy for Decks

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Decks helps you compose photos and videos on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Brian Renshaw does not operate a service that collects your photo library or tracks how you use Decks. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics, or developer-operated crash-reporting service.

Photos and videos

You choose the media you want to use. The system picker can provide selected items without granting access to your whole library. If you enable the integrated Photos browser, Decks uses the Photos access you grant, which may be limited to selected items or include your library. You can change that permission in your device’s Settings.

Decks reads selected media and embedded information such as camera, lens, exposure, capture date, and available Fujifilm settings. It keeps working copies in its own storage. Your original files and Photos assets are not edited. Saving finished copies to Photos requires permission to add images or videos.

Projects, templates, and local data

Projects, imported media, templates, captions, recipes, gear details, logos, and preferences are stored on your device. Templates can contain wording and design assets you include. When you export or share a template, project output, caption, or contact sheet, the recipient receives the information in that file.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Decks stores project and template records and their referenced media in your private CloudKit database, managed by Apple under your Apple account. This makes supported content available on your other devices. It uses your iCloud storage and requires a network connection to transfer changes and original media. Brian does not receive access to that private database.

The separate caption-preset library and local preferences are not a complete synced settings library. Turning off sync stops further synchronization; it does not by itself delete existing cloud records.

Ingest handoffs and Incoming Photos

When you send photographs from Ingest, Decks receives copies of the selected files and associated handoff information. Local sharing uses the system’s file-sharing mechanisms. Supported iCloud handoffs store the transfer files and status in your private Apple cloud account so Decks can receive them on another device. Incoming Photos retains waiting batches until you accept or discard them.

Apple Shortcuts

Decks actions process the files passed to them by your shortcut. A shortcut can apply a saved template, save finished copies to Photos, keep an editable project, or add photographs to Incoming Photos, depending on the action you choose. Other actions you add to a shortcut have their own behavior and permissions.

Export metadata

Export settings control the information embedded in finished files. GPS and contact metadata are excluded by default and can be included through the relevant export settings. Ordinary camera and copyright information may be retained. Text, captions, logos, and camera or recipe details you choose to render are visible in the finished composition, regardless of embedded-metadata choices. Review your output before sharing it.

Apple services and purchases

Decks uses Apple services for optional iCloud features, Photos access, and purchase or entitlement checks where offered. Apple handles App Store and TestFlight distribution and payment information under its own policies. Decks does not receive your payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

You can manage projects and local storage in Decks. Archived and recently deleted projects may retain their media until removed through the app’s available controls. Removing a local download is different from deleting a project or its cloud original. With sync enabled, supported deletions synchronize to your other devices.

Removing the app does not necessarily remove data from iCloud or its shared storage container. Use the app’s deletion controls and your Apple account’s iCloud storage controls to manage retained data.

Feedback

If you email support or send TestFlight feedback, you choose what to share. Your message may include contact information, attachments, device information, and diagnostic information provided by the feedback tool. Send only information you are comfortable sharing.

Children

Decks does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its photo and project data remain under the control of the person using the app and their Apple account.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Decks’s data practices change. Questions: contact@brianrenshaw.app.
