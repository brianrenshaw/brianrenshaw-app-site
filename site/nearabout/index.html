For iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Your lists and routes, with you on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Enter the places
you have in mind.

Type or paste names, addresses, or categories like “coffee shop,” then search and choose the right matches. Pick your current location or another starting point to see driving and walking times and distances. Each place can include its address and links to Apple Maps and Google Maps.

You can still keep the finished list in Notes. Nearabout does the lookups and calculations, then puts the details together for you to copy, share, or use to plan a route.

“Which stop
comes first?”

The list shows how far each place is from your starting point. The route planner helps work out the journey between stops. Choose up to seven, compare your order with the suggested order, and allow time for visits and appointments. Use it for today’s errands or an afternoon with friends.

What have you
got planned?

A vacation you’ve been looking forward to.

Collect the restaurants, coffee shops, and stores you want to visit, and give each its own group. When you’re there, pick a few for the day and let the route planner help with the order.

A friend who trusts your taste.

Put together the places you’d recommend, with a few words about each. When someone asks where to eat, send your list in a helpful text with notes and map links.

Getting to know what’s around you.

Start from where you are or any address you choose. Search the places you want to highlight and get their distances, travel times, and map links together—ready for a visitor, a neighborhood guide, or a social post.

An afternoon with a few stops.

Five errands before pickup. A bar crawl with friends. Or a free afternoon with somewhere good to eat along the way. Put the places together, then work out an order when you’re ready.

Saved lists and routes stay with you across iPhone, iPad, and Mac through iCloud. Share recommendations or a route itinerary as text or PDF, or keep a copy in Notes.
