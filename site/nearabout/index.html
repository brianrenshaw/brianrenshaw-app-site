For iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Keep your lists with you on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

“Where would you
recommend?”

The place you take everyone who visits. The bakery worth getting up early for. Make a list with what to order, when to go, and why you recommend it. When someone asks, you have something personal to send.

  1. 01

    Start with a few places.

    Type or paste names, addresses, or categories like “bakery.” Choose the right locations from the matches.

  2. 02

    Add your notes.

    Add what you’d tell someone about each place. Group your list by restaurants, coffee shops, stores, or whatever makes sense for the plans.

  3. 03

    Share the list.

    Send a list with your notes, addresses, and map links. The person receiving it can open a place right from the list.

What have you
got planned?

A vacation you’ve been looking forward to.

Collect the restaurants, coffee shops, and stores you want to visit, and give each its own group. When you’re there, pick a few for the day and let the route planner help with the order.

A friend who trusts your taste.

Put together the places you’d recommend, with a few words about each. When someone asks where to eat, send your list in a helpful text with notes and map links.

Getting to know what’s around you.

Start from where you are or any address you choose. Make a list of nearby places with distances and travel times—for yourself, for someone visiting, or to share in a post.

An afternoon with a few stops.

Five errands before pickup. A bar crawl with friends. Or a free afternoon with somewhere good to eat along the way. Put the places together, then work out an order when you’re ready.

Saved lists stay with you across iPhone, iPad, and Mac through iCloud. Share them as text or PDF, or keep a copy in Notes.
