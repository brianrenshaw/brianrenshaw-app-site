Nearabout support.

For help or feedback, email contact@brianrenshaw.app. Include your device, Nearabout version, and what you were trying to do.

Start a list

On Home, choose Create a list, then type or paste names, categories, or addresses, one per line. Choose Find places, pick a starting point (your current location or any address), and select the matching locations you want. Check the addresses when a place has more than one branch.

You can use or export the results immediately. Tap the list’s title to name it, or choose Save list, to keep it in your library. Add groups and notes to make it your own.

Share your recommendations

Open Export to choose formatted text, plain text, Markdown, or PDF. Use Include to choose addresses, notes, map links, and travel estimates. You can edit the text before copying, sharing, or saving a file.

Add to Notes uses an Apple Shortcut. Follow the one-time setup in the app, then send your list to Notes with its formatting and map links.

Use your list on another device

Sign in to the same iCloud account on your devices. Saved lists, groups, notes, starting points, and saved routes sync through your private iCloud. Unsaved lists stay on the device where you started them.

Open Settings → Sync Now to check for updates and see the last successful sync. You can also pull to refresh or use Command-R on Mac.

Add travel times or a route

Choose a starting point to compare drive and walk estimates. To visit several places, open Route Planner. Add as many stops as you need, set appointments and time at each place, and compare your order with a suggested order. Travel times are estimates; recalculate a saved route when you need an updated plan.

Unlimited Lists

Nearabout is free with three saved lists, and each list can hold as many places as you like. Unlimited Lists is a one-time purchase that removes the limit on every iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. It is not a subscription.

Open Settings → Unlimited lists to buy it, choose Restore Purchases on a new device, or choose Redeem Code to use an offer code. If you go back to three lists or fewer, nothing is removed; lists above the limit stay available and editable. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Availability and requirements

Nearabout requires iOS 27, iPadOS 27, or macOS 27. It is coming to the App Store; until then you can join the TestFlight beta.

The app includes a searchable guide in Settings → Help & getting started.
