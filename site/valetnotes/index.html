iPhone, iPad & Mac

Park your notes until they’re ready to go.

Valet Notes is a scratchpad with named spaces for quick writing. Leave a note parked as long as you need, then send it where it belongs with one shortcut.

Try Valet Notes on TestFlight

The public beta is open on iPhone and iPad through Apple’s TestFlight app. The Mac app joins the same beta once Apple approves it.

A place for notes in between

Not every note needs a home yet.

Some writing only needs to last a day or two. Picking a folder and a title for each one takes longer than writing it down. Valet Notes gives you a few dedicated spaces to start in. It works alongside Drafts, Bear, Obsidian, iA Writer, and Ulysses, not instead of them.

  1. 1

    Write it down.

    Open a space and start typing. Notes are Markdown, with headings, lists, and checkboxes. Formatting shows as you type, and the symbols appear only where your cursor is.

  2. 2

    Leave it parked.

    Each space has its own name and color. Add more whenever you need them, or delete the ones you don’t use. Your spaces sync between devices with iCloud.

  3. 3

    Send it on.

    Give each space a Space Action: Drafts, Bear, Ulysses, iA Writer, Obsidian, or your task app. Run it, and the note is sent and archived once the app confirms it saved.

Ways to use it

Somewhere to start.

Phone call

Take notes during a call.

Scroll down from the top of your Mac screen and type. When the call ends, press Control–Option–Command–A to send it to Drafts. Valet Notes archives it once Drafts has it.

Task Inbox

Keep a running task inbox.

Add tasks whenever they come up. At the end of the day, send the list to OmniFocus, Things, Todoist, Reminders, or NotePlan, and the inbox is clear for tomorrow.

Today

Keep today’s three things in view.

Make a short checklist in its own space and check items off as you finish them.

Draft

Start a longer piece.

Write the first lines when the idea comes. When you’re ready to keep going, send it to iA Writer, Ulysses, Bear, or Obsidian as a new note.

Project notes

Track feedback while you work.

Note changes and edits without opening another app. When you’re done, append them to the project’s note in Drafts, Bear, Ulysses, iA Writer, or Obsidian.

Reading

Collect quotes as you read.

On iPhone and iPad, highlight a passage in Safari and share it to a space. It’s saved as a Markdown quote with the page title and a link.

On Mac

A drawer at the top of your screen.

Move the pointer to the top edge of the screen and scroll down, or press Control–Option–Command–Space. Up to four spaces open side by side over whatever you’re working on. Swipe to see the rest, or press Command and a space’s number to jump straight to it. Press Escape to close the drawer. Control–Option–Command–A runs your Space Actions from any app, even with the drawer closed.

Also in the app

Small things that help.

Commands

Every action for a note is in Commands. Pin the ones you use, and give them keyboard shortcuts on Mac.

Automatic archiving

Spaces you haven’t touched in 14 days are archived for you. Choose 7 or 30 days, or turn it off.

Browse the archive

Archived notes are dated Markdown files. Search them, copy them, or restore one to an empty space.

Shortcuts and links

Read, add to, or archive spaces from the Shortcuts app, open one with a valetnotes:// link, or show one in a Home Screen widget.
