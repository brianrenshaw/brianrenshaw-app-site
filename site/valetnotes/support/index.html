Valet Notes support

Questions or problems? Email contact@brianrenshaw.app. Include your device and what you were trying to do.

Join the beta

Install Apple’s TestFlight app on your iPhone or iPad, then open the Valet Notes beta invitation and tap Accept. Valet Notes needs iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later. The Mac app, for macOS 14 or later, joins the same invitation once Apple approves its beta.

Open the drawer on Mac

Move the pointer to the top edge of the screen and scroll down, press Control–Option–Command–Space, or click the Valet Notes icon in the menu bar. Press Escape, click outside the drawer, or scroll up over its header to close it. Command–1 through Command–9 jump to a space.

Find an action

Open Commands from the toolbar, or press Command–P with a keyboard. It lists every action for the current note. If you select text first, actions like Archive and Send use only that text. Space Actions always send the whole space.

Sync between devices

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on each device. Your notes, space names, and task settings sync through iCloud. If two devices change the same note before syncing, Valet Notes keeps both versions, separated by a divider.

Get an archived note back

Choose Undo last archive in Commands to restore the most recent one. For older notes, choose Browse archive, find the note, and restore it to an empty space. Archived notes are dated Markdown files, so they are also readable in any text editor. Change automatic archiving, or turn it off, in Settings.

Add, rename, or delete spaces

Open Settings with the gear in the top right, then choose Spaces. Add a space, rename one, or delete one you don’t need. At least one space always stays. A deleted space keeps its writing and settings; bring it back from Spaces → Deleted spaces.

Set up Space Actions

A Space Action sends a whole space to one place you choose. Open Settings → Spaces, choose the space, and pick a destination and action. On Mac, the gear in a space’s header opens its settings directly.

  • Drafts, Bear, and Ulysses: create a new note or sheet, or add to the start or end of one you already have. Paste the existing note’s link or identifier.
  • iA Writer: create a note, or add to one by its library path. Turn on URL commands in iA Writer and save its auth token in Note settings. Each send can be up to 4,000 characters.
  • Obsidian: create a note, append to a note, or append to today’s daily note. Enter the vault name. Daily notes must be turned on in the vault.
  • This space’s task app: send the tasks using the space’s task settings.
  • Markdown archive folder: save a dated Markdown file to a folder you choose, including one in an Obsidian vault.

Run Space Actions from Commands, or press Control–Option–Command–A on Mac. It works from any app while Valet Notes is running. With one space set up, it runs right away. With several, it shows a numbered list; press a number or choose a space. Change the shortcut in Settings → Keyboard.

After the other app confirms it saved the note, Valet Notes archives what was sent and clears the space. Anything you wrote after starting the send stays. If the app never confirms, the note stays put, and the space’s settings let you stop waiting and keep writing.

Send notes to other apps

The other app needs to be installed on the same device. A note stays in its space until the other app confirms it was saved. When sending to Drafts or sending tasks, Valet Notes then archives the sent text. Sending to Apple Notes, Obsidian, or Quick Draft keeps the note in place.

  • Things may ask you to turn on Things URLs the first time.
  • Todoist needs a personal API token, entered in Settings.
  • Reminders asks for permission and saves to the list you choose.
  • Sending to Quick Draft requires Quick Draft Pro.

Each space can use its own task app and syntax. Change them in Settings → Spaces.
