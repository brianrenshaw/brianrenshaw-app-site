For two friends · iPhone and iPad

Nobody has to be the one who cancels.

Some nights you both want to stay home, and neither of you will say it first. In Canceled you each privately say whether you want out. The plan is off only if you both do.

In development for iPhone and iPad. Not yet available to install.

01 / Private

Your choice stays private.

Choose Cancel and nothing changes on your friend’s screen. Their choice is never sent to your device either. You can change your mind right up until you both cancel or the plan starts.

02 / Mutual

Two cancels, and the evening is yours.

When you both choose Cancel, the plan is off and it is revealed to both of you at once. Neither of you had to be the one who asked.

03 / One app

Only one of you needs it.

Send a link and your friend can accept the plan and make their own private choice in a browser. Plans can come from your calendar too. Calendar access is opt-in and starts with nothing selected.

The plan is still on unless you both cancel.

One choice stays private. Two cancels produce a shared cancellation.

The reveal

Your evening has been returned to you.

Nobody sends the awkward text and nobody offers an excuse. The plan simply ends, because you both quietly decided the same thing.

Still being built

Two friends. One mutual understanding.

Canceled is a personal project, still in development for iPhone and iPad. There is nothing to install yet, and no date to promise.
