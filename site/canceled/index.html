Private cancellation choices · iPhone and iPad

Nobody has to be the one who cancels.

You and a friend each privately choose whether to cancel a plan. If you both choose Cancel, the plan is off. If only one of you does, that choice stays private and the plan remains on.

In development for iPhone and iPad. There is nothing to install yet.

01 / Private

Keep your choice private.

Choose Cancel and nothing changes on your friend’s screen. Their choice stays private too. You can change your mind until you both cancel or the plan starts.

02 / Mutual

Cancel when you both agree.

When you both choose Cancel, the plan is canceled and you both find out.

03 / One app

Only one of you needs it.

Send a link so your friend can accept and make their private choice in a browser, without installing the app. You can also add plans from your calendar. Calendar access is opt-in and starts with nothing selected.

The plan is still on unless you both cancel.

One choice stays private. Two cancels produce a shared cancellation.

The reveal

See when a plan is canceled.

A shared cancellation screen confirms that you both chose to cancel.

Messages in the app

A different message when you cancel.

Private cancellations show a message from a shuffled collection of 120 lines. These are examples from the app:

  • The sweatpants lobby has made its opening argument.
  • Your request is with the Ministry of Maybe Not.
  • Your calendar booked the deluxe version of you.
  • The plan sounded different before the shoes became involved.
  • Imagine two people separately discovering the merits of soup.
  • The trailer promised adventure. The audience has notes.

The app also includes messages for keeping plans and viewing past plans.
