Room for the whole reading life

From “you should read this” to the last page.

01 · Remember

Keep what comes next.

Save a title, a series, or something a friend mentioned. Keep their words and their suggested starting point. Arrange your To Read list when you're ready.

Books, people, and recommendations
02 · Read

A little reading counts.

Mark the day and it's recorded. Pages, chapters, percentages or listening position are there if you want them, and off until you do. There's no timer to start.

Recording your reading
03 · Look back

See the habit take shape.

Browse your journal, revisit a finished book, and follow daily consistency alongside monthly and yearly goals you choose.

Understanding your goals

As much detail as you want

Your books. Your way of reading.

Some days, marking the day is enough.

For the rest, there's a place for progress, formats, dates, notes and your own review. Read a favourite again and the earlier reading stays intact, with its own dates and its own record.

Let a longer book count for more toward your goals. A finished reread counts too.

Explore reading history and rereads

A rhythm of your own

Days, months, and a reading year.

A daily habit

See the days you read. Seven consecutive reading days earn one rest day, used automatically when you miss one.

A monthly goal

Choose your target and watch each finished reading contribute. Monthly streaks remember the months you reached it.

A year to look back on

Set an independent yearly target. See weighted progress beside the actual number of readings you finished.

How the numbers work

A library that stays yours

Keep your record close.

Your library lives on your device and syncs through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Export a JSON backup to keep a copy of your own, or CSV for a spreadsheet.

Availability

Reading Habit is in development for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with iOS 27, iPadOS 27 and macOS 27 as the minimum supported versions. A public App Store or TestFlight download is not available yet. This site documents the current development version.
