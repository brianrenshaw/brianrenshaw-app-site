Tracking you can keep up

A little reading counts.

01 · Mark the day

Did you read today?

Tap Read today to record it. However much you read, the day counts. Detailed progress is optional, and there’s no timer to start.

Recording your reading
02 · Follow your habit

See the days you read in your journal. Choose a monthly book goal and watch your finished books add up.

Books, people, and recommendations

See your consistency grow

Build a habit, one day at a time.

A daily habit

See the days you read. Seven consecutive reading days earn one rest day, used automatically when you miss one.

A monthly goal

Choose your target and watch each finished reading contribute. Monthly streaks remember the months you reached it.

A year to look back on

Look back on the books you finished, with a separate yearly goal if you want one.

How the numbers work

As much detail as you want

Keep a record of your reading.

Marking the day is enough.

Add notes or a review when you want to remember more. Reread a favorite and keep each reading’s dates and record.

Pages, chapters, and listening progress are there if you want them. Leave them off when you don’t.

Explore reading history and rereads

A library that stays yours

Keep your record close.

Your library lives on your device and syncs through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Export a JSON backup to keep a copy of your own, or CSV for a spreadsheet.
