01

Open your reports offline.

Keep work dashboards and AI-generated reports on your device. Open them on your iPhone or iPad, even without an internet connection.

02

Keep useful files outside the chat.

Add pages from Files, Google Drive, or another app’s share sheet, or watch a folder so its pages stay current. Organize them with tags and favorites, ready to use again.

03

Use interactive HTML offline.

Import a complete page with its images, scripts, and data. Saved content works offline. Live data and online services still need internet access, which you control per page.

04

Open a page from the Home Screen.

Add a Folio widget in a small or medium size, or a circular one on the Lock Screen, and choose which page it opens. Touch and hold the app icon to jump to a favorite or recent page.

05

Lock access to saved pages.

Require Face ID, or the device passcode, on any page. Locked pages stay protected when opened from a widget or a quick action, and are hidden in the app switcher.

06

Sync your library across devices.

Turn on iCloud sync to keep your library consistent across your iPhone and iPad, with page files and settings as separate choices. It is off until you ask for it, and any page can be left out.
