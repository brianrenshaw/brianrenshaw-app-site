Import a stack of recipe photos with your own AI

Faded Recipes reads a card with Apple Intelligence when you scan it. If you have a shoebox of cards already photographed, you can have an AI you already use read them all, then bring every recipe into Faded at once. Each recipe keeps its photos as the reference card. Faded never connects to your AI account; you hand the AI the photos, and it writes one file that Faded reads.

This works with Claude Code, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any assistant that can read photos. Use the most capable model you have; handwriting is hard, and better models make fewer mistakes.

How it works

  1. Put the photos in one folder

    One photo per card side is fine. The front and back of a card can sit side by side; the AI keeps them together as one recipe.

  2. Give your AI the folder and the prompt

    The prompt below asks it to copy each card exactly and save every recipe in one file, faded-import.json, in the same folder.

  3. Choose the folder in Faded

    On the Mac, choose Add › Import with Your AI › Choose Folder, or File › Import with Your AI. Faded lists the recipes, then adds them all, each marked Check. On iPhone and iPad, see below.

With Claude Code or another agent on your computer

Open the folder of photos in Terminal, start your agent, and paste the prompt. The agent reads each photo, renames it after its recipe (tempting-apple-crisp.jpg), and writes faded-import.json next to them. Then choose that folder in Faded on your Mac, or put it in iCloud Drive and choose it on your iPhone or iPad.

On iPhone and iPad

Choose Add › Import with Your AI, then:

  1. Choose Photos. Pick the card photos. Faded keeps them, numbered in the order you chose them, while you’re in the other app.
  2. Copy Prompt, then Share Photos to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Paste the prompt with the photos and send it. The prompt asks the AI to call the photos 1, 2, 3 in the order attached and to reply with the recipes in one code block.
  3. When the AI answers, copy its whole reply, come back to Faded, and tap Paste. Faded matches each recipe to its photo and adds them all.

If your AI saved photos and a faded-import.json together in Files, choose Use a Folder Instead.

With a chat app on a Mac

Attach the photos and paste the prompt. Save the AI’s reply as a file named faded-import.json in the folder with the same photos, then choose the folder in Faded. Chat apps may rename photos you attach; if a recipe comes in without its photo, ask the AI to use the names of the files you attached.

The prompt

Faded shows the same prompt under Copy Prompt on the Mac. On iPhone and iPad, Copy Prompt gives a version for chat apps that numbers the attached photos instead of naming files.

I'm importing handwritten and printed recipe cards into Faded, a recipe app. Read every photo of a recipe in the folder I point you to. Use each photo's exact file name.

Transcribe each recipe exactly as it is written. This is copying, not cooking:
- Copy every word, abbreviation, amount, fraction, unit, oven temperature, and time as written ("1/2 c. oleo", not "1/2 cup margarine"). Keep the writer's spelling.
- Never add, complete, correct, or guess an ingredient, amount, or step. If something can't be read, write [unclear] in its place.
- One ingredient per line, in the order written, with its amount and preparation. Put a heading written over a group of ingredients ("Filling") in "section".
- A food measured only inside the directions goes at the end of the ingredients.
- Steps in order, wrapped lines joined. Notes are any other handwritten remarks.
- Make the capitalization consistent, whatever the card did (all capitals included):
  - Titles and section headings in title case: "Tempting Apple Crisp", "Topping".
  - Ingredient lines in lowercase: "1/2 c. firmly packed brown sugar". Keep brand and proper names capitalized ("Rice Krispies", "Crisco", "Grandma's"). Keep every unit abbreviation exactly as written, because its case can be its meaning: "T." is a tablespoon and "t." a teaspoon, so never change "1 T. butter" to "1 t. butter".
  - Steps and notes in sentence case: "Bake at 375° for 30 minutes."
  Capitalization is the only thing to change; keep the words, spelling, and abbreviations.
- Leave out printed form captions such as "Recipe from the kitchen of" unless something is handwritten after them.
- When several photos show one recipe (front and back, or two pages), make one recipe that lists all of them, front first. When one photo holds two recipes, make two recipes that both list it.
- Skip photos that aren't recipes.
- If you can rename files, rename each photo after its recipe's title in lowercase with hyphens, keeping its extension: "tempting-apple-crisp.jpg", then "tempting-apple-crisp-2.jpg" for a second photo of the same recipe. List the new names in "photos". If you can't rename files, list the names as they are.

Write the result as JSON in exactly this shape, and nothing else. When you can write files, save it as faded-import.json in the same folder as the photos.

{
  "format": "faded-import",
  "version": 1,
  "recipes": [
    {
      "photos": ["apple-crisp.jpg", "apple-crisp-2.jpg"],
      "title": "Apple Crisp",
      "from": "Grandma Ruth",
      "servings": 6,
      "yield": "",
      "prepMinutes": 0,
      "cookMinutes": 30,
      "totalMinutes": 0,
      "ingredients": [
        { "section": "", "line": "4 c. sliced apples" },
        { "section": "Topping", "line": "1/2 c. brown sugar" }
      ],
      "steps": ["Place apples in shallow baking dish.", "Bake at 375° for 30 minutes."],
      "notes": ""
    }
  ]
}

Use "" or 0 for anything the card doesn't say.

Check what the AI read

Even the best AI misreads some handwriting. Every imported recipe is marked Check, so you can open it next to its photo and fix a line. Anything the AI couldn’t read is marked [unclear].

Get your whole library out

Choose Sort & View › Export Library, or File › Export Library on the Mac, to save a folder with every recipe. It holds each recipe as a Markdown file you can open anywhere, every reference photo as a JPEG, a Faded recipe file for each recipe, and a faded-import.json. Choose that folder with Import with Your AI to bring the whole library back into Faded.

Privacy

Faded doesn’t send your photos to any AI for this. You choose the AI and give it the photos yourself, and that service’s own privacy terms apply. Read the Faded Recipes privacy policy
