Faded Recipes privacy policy

Effective November 28, 2026. Applies to iPhone, iPad, Mac, and the TestFlight beta.

Recipe storage and iCloud

Your device stores your recipes, photos, contributor names, tags, notes, and source links. If you enable iCloud for Faded Recipes, Apple syncs them through your private iCloud database. Cooking progress stays on each device. The app has no accounts or developer-run server. I can’t see your recipe library. Apple’s iCloud data security guide

Photos and text

The app requests camera access to scan cards. When you import from Photos, it can access only the images you select. It uses Apple text recognition and, on supported devices, Apple Intelligence.

Some devices send scans or pasted text to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute for processing. The review screen shows when this happens. How Apple protects these requests

Voice recordings

Read a Recipe uses the microphone to record temporary audio. Apple transcribes it on your device and may download language support first. The app deletes the audio after transcription or when you discard it. If transcription fails, you can retry or discard the recording.

You can edit the transcript. When you turn it into a recipe, the app processes it like pasted text.

Website imports

When you import a link, the site and the services it loads receive connection details, including your IP address. The app saves the recipe and source link and discards cookies. Instagram captions and screenshots require no sign-in.

Imports with your own AI

You share selected photos directly with your AI provider under its privacy terms. Faded Recipes imports the reply you paste or the files in the folder you select. It never connects to your AI account.

Sharing and backups

You choose who receives a shared recipe. A Faded Recipes file can include the original photos. Export Library saves all recipes and photos to a folder you choose.

Deleting recipes

Deleted recipes stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days across synced devices. After 30 days, or when you choose Delete Now, the app permanently deletes the recipe and its saved photos.

Uninstalling removes local data. Recipes in iCloud return when you reinstall. To erase them from iCloud, delete the recipes and empty Recently Deleted, or remove the app’s data in iCloud storage settings. Copies in Photos, exports, backups, and shared files remain.

Tracking and contact

The app has no ads or analytics. Apple may share TestFlight feedback and crash reports you choose to send under its terms.

For privacy questions, email contact@brianrenshaw.app. I use your address, message, and attachments only to reply.
