Faded Recipes privacy policy

Effective November 27, 2026. This covers the Faded Recipes app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, including the TestFlight beta. I make the app on my own, and I don’t collect anything from it.

Your recipes stay on your device

Your recipe box lives on your device: the recipes, photos of your cards, who each recipe is from, tags, notes, source links, and where you left off cooking. You don’t need an account. I don’t run a server, so I never see your recipes.

How the app reads a photo or pasted text

The app asks for the camera so you can photograph a card. If you import from Photos, it gets only the pictures you pick. It reads them with Apple’s text recognition and, when your device supports it, Apple Intelligence.

On some devices, a scan or pasted text is sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute to be read. The review screen tells you when that happened. Apple explains how those requests are protected in its Private Cloud Compute overview.

Recording a spoken recipe

When you choose Read a Recipe, the app asks for microphone access and records a temporary audio file on your device. Apple’s speech recognition transcribes it on your device and may first download language support. The app removes the audio after successful transcription or when you discard the recording. If transcription fails, you can retry or discard it.

You can edit the transcript before asking the app to read it as a recipe. At that point the same text processing described above applies.

Importing a recipe from a website

When you paste a link, your device loads that page, the same as opening it in Safari. The website, and anything it loads, sees ordinary connection details like your IP address. The app saves the recipe and the link and doesn’t keep any cookies from the visit. Pasting an Instagram caption or importing a screenshot doesn’t require signing in to Instagram or any other app.

Sharing, backups, and deleting

When you share a recipe, you pick where it goes. A Faded recipe file can include the photos of the card, and whoever you send it to gets that copy.

Deleting a recipe deletes its saved photos too. Deleting the app deletes your recipe box. Pictures in your photo library, copies you’ve sent to people, and device backups are separate and aren’t affected.

No ads or tracking

There are no ads and no analytics in the app. If you’re testing through TestFlight, Apple may pass along feedback and crash reports you choose to send, under Apple’s terms. If you email me, I get your address, your message, and anything you attach, and I use them only to reply.
