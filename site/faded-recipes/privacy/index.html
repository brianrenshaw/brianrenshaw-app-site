Faded Recipes privacy policy

Effective November 27, 2026. This policy covers the Faded Recipes app for iPhone and iPad, including the current TestFlight beta.

Where your recipes are stored

Your recipe box is stored on your device. It can include recipe text, photos of cards, names, tags, notes, source links, and cooking progress. You can use the app without an account. Brian Renshaw does not maintain a server containing your recipe box.

How photos and text are read

Camera access lets you photograph a card. When you import from Photos, the app receives the images you select. Faded Recipes uses Apple’s text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence to read and organize a recipe.

On supported devices, recipe text or a scan can be sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute for processing. The review screen identifies when Apple’s cloud model was used. Apple describes how it protects requests in its Private Cloud Compute overview.

What happens when you import a website

Your device requests the page at the URL you provide. The website and any services it loads can receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Faded Recipes uses a temporary web session and saves the recipe and its source link in your box. Pasting a social caption or importing a screenshot does not require connecting your social account.

Sharing, backups, and deletion

When you share a recipe, you choose the destination in the system share sheet. A Faded recipe file can include scanned pages. The recipient or service you choose receives that copy.

You can delete a recipe and its saved scans from the app. Deleting the app removes its local library. Photos already in your photo library, copies you have shared, and device backups are managed separately.

Analytics and support

Faded Recipes has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple may provide beta feedback and diagnostic information through TestFlight under Apple’s terms. If you email support, Brian receives the email address, message, and attachments you send and uses them to respond.
