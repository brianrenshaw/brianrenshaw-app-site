Save your family’s handwritten recipe cards

Photograph a handwritten card or record someone talking you through a family recipe. Faded Recipes turns it into ingredients and directions you can cook from. With a scan, the original handwriting stays a tap away.

Coming soon to the App StoreHow it works ↓

Free for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No subscription, ads, or account.

From a stack of cards to a recipe box

  1. Scan the cards

    Photograph handwritten cards or printed cookbook pages, or choose photos you already have. Keep the front and back together, or import a stack as separate recipes.

  2. Check the handwriting

    Keep scanning while the app reads earlier cards. Open a recipe marked for review to compare it with the original. Zoom in, crop to one recipe, or correct an ingredient or step.

  3. File the recipe

    Add who it came from and tags like “Sunday dinner.” File your box by name, category, person, or date added. Choose whether the cards show cooking time, servings, or ingredient count.

Save a recipe someone tells you

Choose Read a Recipe and let them talk you through it. Stop the recording, check the transcript, and turn it into ingredients and steps. You can also type, paste, or dictate straight into the text editor.

Bring in a recipe from the web

Paste a recipe link to pull in the ingredients and directions and keep a link to the source. For an Instagram recipe, paste the caption or import a screenshot.

Cook from your recipe box

Find the card you remember

Search for a title, a person, a tag, or an ingredient. If you remember Grandma made something with buttermilk, try both words.

Change the batch size

Choose a scale from ¼× to 4×. Ingredient amounts update in the list and in the directions, so you can see how much goes into the bowl at each step.

Keep your place

Check off ingredients as you add them and mark each step when you finish. The recipe remembers your progress if you leave and come back.

Find an ingredient swap

Choose an ingredient to see suggested substitutes for what you’re missing or for a dietary need. Save a suggestion in the recipe notes for next time.

Import a shoebox of photos

Already photographed a stack of cards? Have ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or an AI agent like Claude Code read them with a prompt from the app, then bring every recipe in at once with its photos. How to import with your own AI

Get everything out

Export Library saves a folder with every recipe as Markdown, every reference photo as a JPEG, and Faded files that bring the whole library back in.

Pass a recipe along

Send a Faded Recipes file so someone can open their own copy with the original scans. Share a printable PDF, plain text, or Markdown. To export several recipes as Markdown, select them together in the recipe box.

Keep a private recipe box

Your recipe box, including the photos of your cards, syncs between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac through your own iCloud account. There’s no Faded account, and I never see your recipes. Recording uses on-device transcription where supported. When available, Apple’s Private Cloud Compute can help read a difficult scan or turn text into a recipe. Read the privacy policy
