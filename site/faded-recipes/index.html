How to save a recipe

  1. Scan or import photos

    Photograph both sides of a card or select photos from your library. You can import several recipes at once.

  2. Check the text

    Compare the ingredients and steps with the photo. Zoom in to check an amount or correct a word.

  3. Organize your recipes

    Add tags and the name of the person who gave you the recipe. Search by title, person, tag, or ingredient.

Record a spoken recipe

Choose Read a Recipe to record someone. Review the transcript before saving the ingredients and steps. You can also type or paste a recipe.

Save a recipe from the web

Paste a link to save the recipe and its source. For Instagram, paste the caption or import a screenshot.

Cook from a saved recipe

Adjust the servings

Scale a recipe from ¼× to 4×. Amounts update in the ingredient list and directions.

Track your progress

Check off ingredients and steps. Your place is saved on that device.

Substitute an ingredient

Choose an ingredient to see alternatives. Save a substitute in your recipe notes.

Questions

What if the app misreads a card?

Faded Recipes can miss words or amounts, especially in faded or cursive handwriting. Edit the text, read the card aloud, or import the photos with your own AI.

Where are my recipes stored?

Recipes and photos stay on your device. Turn on iCloud for Faded Recipes to sync them across devices. Privacy policy

How do I share or export recipes?

Share a PDF, plain text, Markdown, or a Faded Recipes file with the original photos. Choose Export Library to back up every recipe and photo.
