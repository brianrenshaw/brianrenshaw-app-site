Find a recipe by name, or by who gave it to you.

File Grandma’s biscuits under Bread. Keep her name on the recipe and her tip about the biscuit cutter in the notes. Next time, search for Grandma and the recipe is right there.

In the kitchen, cross off ingredients and mark each step as you go. You can go back to the box, open the recipe again, and pick up where you left off.

Add the recipes you already cook.

From a paper card

Photograph the card as it is. Crop it afterward, compare the transcription with the photo, and correct a quantity or a name. The scanned page stays with the recipe.

From a recipe website

Paste the page link. Faded Recipes pulls out the ingredients and directions and keeps a link to the source. Review the result before filing it.

From a social post

Copy recipe text from an Instagram caption or another post and paste it into Faded Recipes. If the recipe is written on the image, import a screenshot instead.
