Keep your family’s handwritten recipes, and the cards they came on.

Grandma’s biscuit recipe is on an index card with a butter stain and pencil that gets lighter every year. Take a picture of it. The app types out the ingredients and directions so you can cook from your phone, and it keeps the photo so her handwriting is still there.

See the recipe box ↓

Free on iPhone and iPad. No subscription and no ads. In private beta right now.

Find a recipe by name, or by who gave it to you.

Each recipe can say where it came from: Grandma June, Aunt Carol, a cookbook, a website. Search for “Grandma” and everything she gave you comes up. Her note about not twisting the cutter stays with the biscuits.

While you cook, cross off ingredients as they go in the bowl and check off each step. If you leave to look at another recipe, your place is still marked when you come back.

Add recipes from old cards, websites, and Instagram.

From a recipe card

Take a photo, or pick one you already have. Check what the app read against the card, fix anything it got wrong, and save. The photo stays with the recipe.

From a recipe website

Paste the link. You get the ingredients and directions without the long story and the pop-ups, plus a link back to the page you found it on.

From Instagram and other apps

Most recipes on Instagram are in the caption. Copy it and paste it in. If the recipe is written on the picture, take a screenshot and import that instead.

It holds your recipes and nothing else.

There’s no feed of recipes to try, no meal planner, no shopping list, and no account to make. Faded Recipes is a box for the recipes your family actually cooks, and it’s free.
