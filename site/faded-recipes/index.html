Old recipe cards are hard to read and easy to lose

A lot of family recipes are written on index cards or notebook pages, and many of them are the only copy. If you type one into a notes app, you keep the words but lose the card. Faded Recipes keeps the typed recipe and the photo of the card together.

How to save a recipe card

  1. Take a photo

    Photograph the card, or choose a photo you already have. A card with writing on both sides is saved as one recipe.

  2. Check the text

    The app reads the handwriting and shows the text next to the photo. Correct anything it misread, then save.

  3. File it

    Add who the recipe came from and a category, like Dessert or Dinner. It goes into your recipe box, filed A to Z.

Keep all your recipes in one place

Search by name or by person

Type a recipe name, or the name of the person who gave it to you.

Cook from your phone

Cross off ingredients and mark each step as you go. Scale a recipe to make a half or double batch.

Save recipes from websites and Instagram

Paste a recipe link and the app pulls out the ingredients and directions. For Instagram, paste the caption or import a screenshot.

Share a recipe with family

Send a recipe as a Faded Recipes file with the card photos, a PDF, or plain text.

Your recipes stay on your device

You don’t need an account, and your recipes aren’t stored on a server. Handwriting is read with Apple’s text recognition and Apple Intelligence. Read the privacy policy

No meal planning or recipe suggestions

Faded Recipes doesn’t plan meals, make shopping lists, or show you recipes from other people. It holds only the recipes you add.
