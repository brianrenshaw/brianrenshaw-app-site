Ingest/Keyboard shortcuts/Marking

Marking

By default a marking key acts where you are and does not move on. Turn on advancing in Settings → Browsing, and then holding ⇧ does the opposite for one press. The command names in the app flip to match, so the menu always tells the truth.

no keyClear all tags, with a confirmation

Tag, rating, label, and review actions have separate advance settings. With multiple photos selected, T tags all of them, or clears their tags when all are already tagged. Viewing alone does not mark a photo reviewed. Protected photographs are skipped with an explanation.

A mark applies to every selected photograph, or to the one under the focus ring. On a collapsed bracket stack it applies to every frame in the stack.
