Ingest/Keyboard shortcuts/Video

Video

These work while a video is open in the preview, and they take precedence over the letters they share.

KeysCommand
Space or KPlay or pause
J LShuttle backwards and forwards, faster each press
, .Step one frame back and forward
← →Step a frame. Hold ⇧ to jump seconds.
MMute
↑ ↓Move to the previous or next item, so a video never traps you
