Ingest/Keyboard shortcuts/Moving

Moving

KeysCommand
→ ←Next and previous photograph
Home EndFirst and last photograph
⌘[ ⌘]Back and forward through the sources you have opened
⌥⌘1Put the keyboard in the sources list. Esc or ← returns to the photographs.
⌥⌘2Put the keyboard back on the photographs
⌥⌘3Put the keyboard in the inspector

In the sources list, ↑ and ↓ move, ↩ or → opens, and typing the first letters of a name jumps to it.
