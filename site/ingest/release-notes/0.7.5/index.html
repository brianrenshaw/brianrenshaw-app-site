What’s new in Ingest

Ingest 0.7.5

Recipe controls within reach

  • Keep Copy, Export, Edit, the simulation keyword action, and queued-change controls above the recipe details.
  • Keep Save, Cancel, and Use Camera Settings above the editor.
  • Choose Don’t show again in the after-ingest dialog to skip future card recipe confirmations. The choice is remembered only after a change is successfully queued; Cancel leaves it unchanged.
  • Turn confirmations back on in Settings → Sidebar Information → Fujifilm Recipe.

Read the recipe guide.
