What’s new in Ingest

Ingest 0.7.0

Apple Photos, integrated and optional

  • Browse the System Photo Library, favorites, ordinary albums, and Unfiled and Unreviewed inboxes. Search albums, preview photos, and review selections with the keyboard.
  • Create and organize albums and folders, file photos into several albums, adjust order, and preview additive grouping recipes before applying them.
  • Edit native favorites, dates, and locations. Native captions, keywords, and ratings are available on macOS 27; earlier systems retain local ratings. Tags, colors, and review marks stay local.
  • Send JPEGs or JPEG companions from file workflows through a reviewed import sheet, with explicit RAW options and content receipts that reuse confirmed imports. Interrupted submissions require reconciliation or an intentional retry.
  • Bring Photos selections into Social Export using retained copies of the current rendered images, and send completed Social JPEGs back to Photos.
  • Keep Apple Photos integration on by default, or turn it off in Settings → General to hide its source, commands, and actions. Permission is requested when connecting or sending.
  • Keep album removal separate from confirmed library deletion. Photos changes follow your iCloud settings.

This remains a working beta. Shared Album publishing, Smart Album rule editing, and continuous rule synchronization are not included. Read the Photos guide and updated privacy details.
