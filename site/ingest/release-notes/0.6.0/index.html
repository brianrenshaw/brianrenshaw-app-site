What’s new in Ingest

Ingest 0.6.0

RAW histogram and Photo Info

  • Inspect supported RAW sensor data with highlight headroom in stops and channel clipping information. Unreadable RAW files fall back to a labeled JPEG histogram.
  • Switch between RAW and JPEG histograms, choose RGB or individual channels, select a linear or stops scale for RAW data, and mark clipped regions on the image.
  • Render a RAW preview for one photo on demand, or compare its RAW and JPEG previews side by side with linked zoom and pan.
  • Comparison zoom aligns immediately on double-click and preview labels track completed rendering.
  • Photo context menus offer Show in Finder, Copy File Path, and Copy Filename.
  • The full metadata editor includes structured IPTC fields, guided templates, and reversible crops. Specialized fields stay out of the default sidebar.

Learn how the RAW histogram and comparison controls work.

Photo Info now keeps Caption, Keywords, and Location close at hand. Choose and reorder sidebar information in Settings. Edit and approve on-device Apple Intelligence caption and keyword suggestions on supported macOS 27 systems, and enter location by address, coordinates, or city/state.
