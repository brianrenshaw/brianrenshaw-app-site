What’s new in Ingest

Ingest 0.5.0

A faster way to browse folders, a simpler tagging workflow, and more control over Social Export.

Browse and review

  • Browse your Mac's folder tree inside Ingest, search folders, and open a folder with a single selection. Leaf folders no longer show misleading disclosure arrows.
  • Show photos in subfolders when you need them. The visible toggle defaults off, and selecting a photo identifies its containing folder in the tree.
  • Open a photo from Finder with Ingest to jump to its folder and select it.
  • Zoom the grid all the way to one photo. Improved pinch sizing keeps cached previews visible; arrow navigation stays with photos by default.
  • Restore compact Grid, Preview, and Split icons. Press I or use the info button to cycle basic, full, or hidden photo information in all three views.
  • Inspect RGB or luminance histograms. Histogram, Camera Details, Keywords, Caption, and Location open expanded and remember later disclosure choices.
  • Metadata shortcuts reveal, scroll to, and focus their fields. Location updates preserve the current view, selection, zoom, and cached photos instead of reopening the folder.
  • Clear an active filter from the folder header. Command descriptions explain overlays and navigation; AF Point Overlay reports when focus-point information is unavailable.

Tagging and deletion

  • Use Tag/Untag as a simple flag: T toggles and U untags. Stars, colors, and keywords stay independent. Reject and its shortcuts, filters, badges, and cleanup action have been removed; X is unassigned.
  • All includes every photo in the current scope; Untagged includes every photo without a tag. Unmarked describes photos without tags, stars, or colors.
  • Delete selected photos with the Delete key or a context menu. Confirmation is on by default, with a preference available both in Settings and in the confirmation dialog.

Organize

  • Group by Tagged/Untagged, star rating, color label, and the existing date, camera, lens, file type, and keyword choices. Grouping is available in the initial setup and inspector.
  • A clearer inspector follows Grouping, Destination, Filenames, and Destination Preview. Source-folder subfolders are the default; alternate destinations show their folder chooser explicitly.
  • Preview full paths when useful and see three example filenames while renaming. Advanced date options stay out of the main flow.
  • Review & Organize is a prominent fixed action, with Copy, Move, or Rename identified before execution. Background planning keeps previews stable while typing.

Social Export

  • Choose Fit or Fill for a photo, drag its position, and zoom within its cell. Grid cells support common and custom aspect ratios independently of the canvas, with remaining space kept as background.
  • Add editable text layers to individual frames with fonts, alignment, color, and optional backgrounds. Copy a text design to all frames, optionally including its wording.
  • Snap photos and text to centers and edges. An optional approximate Stories safe-area guide appears only on 9:16 still-image canvases and is never included in exported files.

A quieter native interface

  • A wider resizable sidebar keeps folder names readable. Favorites, Recent, and Saved Views collapse independently and remember their state.
  • Compact photo controls combine view, sizing, sort, and filters; secondary controls move into an overflow menu at narrow widths.
  • Archivo headings retain Ingest's character, with more readable system-font controls and restrained orange accents. Browse, Organize, and Social Export have distinct active colors, and a labeled Ingest… button opens the import flow.
  • Removed the oversized orange pane-focus frame while retaining focus feedback on individual controls.

Existing IPTC capabilities are unchanged in this release. Requires macOS 15 or later and Apple silicon.
