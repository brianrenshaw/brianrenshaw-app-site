My workspace or filenames are not right

  1. Confirm the active workspace in the window toolbar. Open at Launch is a separate choice.
  2. Open its setup and check required questions, the receiving folder, and File Names. New workspaces keep original filenames until you change that choice.
  3. Connect an external destination. If its drive was renamed, reselect the folder.
  4. Use Review & Save to inspect the examples, save the workspace, then check the actual output paths in Import Settings.

For continuing group numbers, choose the same shoot across cards. A new shoot starts fresh; failed or partial imports may leave reserved-number gaps. Workspace Builder · Naming reference.
