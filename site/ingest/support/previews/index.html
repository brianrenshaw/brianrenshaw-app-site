Thumbnails are slow or missing

Preview speed depends on the camera format, available embedded image, storage, and connection. If browsing is slow, try a local copy to distinguish file-rendering work from a slow card reader, network volume, or file provider.

Thumbnails that are expensive to build are cached on disk, under 2 GB, and cleared from Settings, Advanced. Clearing the cache is safe and never touches a photograph.

A photograph with no thumbnail at all is usually a raw format Ingest cannot read yet. See cameras and files it reads.

Adobe preview is unavailable

Select a RAW photo from a card or folder, then run Preview with Adobe Edits. Install Adobe DNG Converter or use Choose Converter… to locate it. If conversion fails for your camera, use Get Latest Converter… in the preview notice and try again.

Adobe edits are missing or outdated

Save the edits to the photo or its sidecar in your Adobe app; Lightroom catalog-only edits are not visible to Ingest. Run Preview with Adobe Edits again to refresh. Show Camera Preview returns to the embedded camera image. Adobe preview guide →
