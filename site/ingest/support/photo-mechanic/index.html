Coming from Photo Mechanic

The habits carry over. T tags, the arrow keys move, ⌘G is ingest, and {seqn} is the saved sequence counter. The naming reference lists supported brace tokens; check your existing patterns against it.

Three differences worth knowing. Marking keys do not advance by default, and you can turn advancing on in Settings → Browsing. Tags are a simple on/off flag, independent of ratings and colors. The Filter panel offers All, Tagged, and Untagged, and the active filter can be cleared from the folder header. And stars and color labels use the Lightroom keys, 1 to 5 and 6 to 9, saved in supported files or XMP sidecars so compatible editing apps can read them.

Settings, Shortcuts lists every command. Click a shortcut or focus it with Tab and press Return to record a new one. Navigation keys and standard Mac editing shortcuts stay fixed; Settings explains any conflict.
