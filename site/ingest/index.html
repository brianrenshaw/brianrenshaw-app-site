01 / Workspaces

Set up the shoot once.
Forget the folders.

Workspaces are not just metadata presets. They remember the shoot setup — wedding, wildlife, headshots, personal — so you configure once and stop thinking about folder structure.

  • Metadata templates, folder structures, and renaming rules
  • Empty delivery folders created alongside your originals, ready for exports from Lightroom
  • Apple Photos and Social Export on or off for each workspace
  • Your starting view: grid, photo-first, or split
  • Grouping for bursts and multiple exposures

02 / Card to folders

From the card.
To your folders.

Open a card or a folder. Tag keepers with the keyboard. Ingest copies to your destinations and verifies the copies against the source. Keep Originals leaves the card untouched.

  1. Open. Card or folder. No catalog to build first.
  2. Decide. Your ratings, your calls.
  3. File. Destinations you choose. Copies verified.

Originals are kept by default. Source cleanup is a separate, explicit choice after verified copying. You can simply open a folder; no workspace setup required.

Your naming rules and folder templates travel with the workspace — curly braces for variables, semicolon for metadata fields. Explore naming and metadata →

03 / Command Palette

Keyboard-first.
Never lost.

⌘K is the safety net and the teacher in one. Search an action, view, or mode. The shortcut appears beside it so you learn as you go.

Forgot the crop shortcut? Type “crop,” run it, and see the key.

Need more room? Find Hide Right Sidebar once; let muscle memory take over.

Want the photo front and center? Find Focus Mode, or press F to hide the surrounding controls. Press it again to restore them.

Looking for a folder? Search for Find Folder without leaving the keyboard.

Power when you want it.
A map when you don’t.

04 / Metadata Assist

Type ;.
See your options.

A structured editor, not an AI writer. Type ; and pick the field — Location, Caption, Keywords, or anything else — then Tab and fill it in. Changes show in a review panel first. Typing alone writes nothing. Or copy metadata from one photo and paste it onto many with ⇧⌘C / ⇧⌘V.

Reuse captions and keywords across a shoot, stage shared import details, and keep place names separate from GPS until you confirm a pin. How Metadata Assist works →
