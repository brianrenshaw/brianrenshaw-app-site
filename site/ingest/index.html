The command bar

Find and run commands with ⌘K.

Type a few letters to find a command, favorite folder, preset, or workspace. Use the arrow keys to select a result and Return to open or run it.

Each command shows its keyboard shortcut. You can pin frequently used commands to the top of the list.

Import

Import photos with names and metadata.

Choose which photos to import and where to save them. Preview their filenames and select a metadata preset before copying. Ingest verifies each copy against its source, then applies names and metadata.

Save your destinations and naming preferences so they are ready for the next shoot.

A closer look at importing →

Cull

Compare, rate, and select photos.

Browse quickly, compare similar frames side by side, and make your selections with the keyboard. Tag, rate, and label as you go.

Zoom and pan stay synchronized between photos in Compare view. Filter by tags or ratings to review your selections.

RAW histogram and preview comparison

Inspect exposure directly from supported RAW sensor data, with highlight headroom in stops and channel clipping information. Switch between RAW and JPEG histograms, render a RAW preview on demand, or compare RAW and JPEG previews side by side.

Manage

Organize photos in existing folders.

Choose Copy, Move, or Rename, then start with no grouping or a date, camera, file type, or saved recipe. Preview the filenames and folders before applying changes. Using Organize →

Return to a saved setup automatically, or choose Start Over to make a different arrangement. Browse keeps your selection and filters while you work.

Browse, Organize, and Social Export are always a click away at the top of the window.

Social Export

Make a frame. Share a set.

Turn selected photos into single-photo frames or collages. Choose a layout and it updates immediately. Adjust crops, margins, borders, and backgrounds while watching the preview change.

Click Export to choose a folder and image size, then review filenames before saving JPEG or PNG images. Original photos stay unchanged.

Return to a project when you need it. Archive or delete the rest, or let inactive projects clean themselves up. Keep protects the ones you want to save.

Apple Photos

Your library is another source.

Your System Photo Library sits in the sources list under your folders. Choose Library, Favorites, Unfiled, Unreviewed, or an album and the photographs swap in place. The window does not change, the sidebar keeps its width, and the tab you were on stays where it was.

Everything you already know still works. The same keys, the same Grid, Preview, and Split, the same right-hand inspector, the same sort and filter bar. A command that cannot apply to a Photos album says so rather than quietly disappearing.

Work through Unfiled and Unreviewed, mark favorites, and file a selection into several albums at once. Send JPEGs from your file workflow into Photos, or bring a Photos selection into Social Export.

On by default, with a switch in Settings to turn it off, and then it is simply absent. Photos access is requested when you connect, not at launch. Native caption, keyword, and rating edits require macOS 27.

About Ingest

I built Ingest because I wanted a modern Mac app for the part of the workflow Photo Mechanic has covered for years. Importing, culling, renaming, and getting to the edit. Quick previews for Sony ARW and Fujifilm RAF, a command bar, and a build for Apple silicon.

There’s a place for AI culling, and the tools built for it are good at it. It just isn’t what everyone needs. Ingest assumes you’d rather make the selects yourself, and it’s built to keep up with you.

  • You make the selects. Every tag, rating, and color label is your choice.
  • Copies checked against the source. How verification works →
  • Card metadata and filenames stay untouched. How cards are protected →
  • No account or analytics. Optional Apple Photos follows your iCloud settings. Read the privacy details →
