Apple Photos, inside Ingest

Connect your library

Choose Apple Photos in Sources and allow Photos access. Ingest uses your System Photo Library, not an arbitrary library file.

Integration is optional. Enable it in both Settings → General and the current workspace. Turning it off hides Photos controls but retains your saved review state and recipes. Finish active Photos operations first.

Find and browse photos

  • Choose Library, Favorites, or an album. Find albums filters the sidebar.
  • Unfiled shows photos outside ordinary albums; Unreviewed tracks your Ingest review progress.
  • Search by filename. Caption and keyword search requires macOS 27; the first library search may take time.
  • Choose Grid, Preview, or Split with ⌘1, ⌘2, or ⌘3. Space toggles Preview. Command-click or Shift-click selects several photos.

Work offline

Local photos work offline. Originals stored only in iCloud must download through Photos; a thumbnail may appear first. For offline access, choose Download Originals to this Mac in Photos → Settings → iCloud and let syncing finish. This uses more storage.

Review and file a selection

  • G: add selected photos to albums.
  • R: mark reviewed; ⇧R: mark and advance.
  • F: toggle a native Photos favorite in this context.
  • ⌥⌘S and ⌘I: toggle Sources and Inspector.

Tags, color labels, and reviewed marks stay in Ingest. Analysis starts when you choose Analyze Review Photos. The inspector loads detail as needed; its histogram describes the rendered Photos image.

Manage albums

Use the plus menu to create albums or folders, and context menus to rename or move them. In editable albums, ⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→ reorder selected photos.

Delete removes photos from the current album. Delete from Library… is a separate confirmed action affecting every album, Recently Deleted, and iCloud Photos.

Edit metadata and organize

Press M to review metadata edits. Captions, keywords, and native star ratings require macOS 27. Favorites, capture dates, and locations work on earlier supported versions. Earlier-version ratings stay local; transferring them into Photos later requires review.

Organize Photos into Albums previews grouping, destination paths, assignments, and order. Apply adds to those albums without removing unrelated contents. Recipes run on demand; Shared Album publishing and native Smart Album rule editing are not included.

Send files to Apple Photos

  1. Select photos in a writable folder and choose Send to Apple Photos….
  2. Review the destination album and format. The default uses JPEGs or JPEG companions and skips files without one. Choose RAW explicitly if wanted; RAW + JPEG creates separate Photos assets.
  3. Confirm the reviewed import. Ingest stages verified copies and leaves source files unchanged.

Memory cards must finish import and backups first. Organize offers Send Groups to Photos…; after-import actions use the same reviewed flow.

Check imports and duplicates

Receipts recognize previous Ingest imports by content hash; they do not scan your whole Photos library for duplicates. Use Photos Import History to reconcile uncertain results. Allow a New Import deliberately retries and may create a duplicate. A successful import does not confirm iCloud upload.

Send to Decks

Select stills and choose Send to Decks… (⇧⌘E). Ingest prepares the rendered images, downloading if needed, for a new or existing Decks project.
