Property outlines

Show the property on an aerial photo.

Click around the lot to draw an outline. Choose the line color and style, tint the inside, or make the surrounding area black and white, darker, or blurred.

Save an outlined copy alongside the original. Reopen it to adjust the line or styling later.

Markers

Add labels, pins, and arrows.

Mark the home, label a feature, or point toward something outside the frame. Move and resize markers on the photo, rotate arrows, and add a compass with an adjustable north direction.

Use Find Place to search Apple Maps and add a place name with an estimated drive or walk time.

How photo markers work →

Nearby places

Include what’s near the listing.

Paste a list of places and search near the listing address. Review the matches and Apple Maps travel estimates, then copy the list as text.

With an outline open, add the places to the photo as one card or separate labels. Choose driving or walking times and optional distances.

Build a nearby-places list →

Crop and straighten

Adjust the framing before delivery.

Choose an aspect ratio, adjust the crop, and straighten the photo. Rotate in quarter turns or flip it horizontally.

How to crop a photo →

Keep the original photo.

Saving a crop creates a new JPG that takes the original’s place in the job. The original moves to Excluded and stays on disk. Select the cropped copy to edit it again using the original image.

Outlines and crops render new images. Rename, Copy, and Move preserve the image data of the files you process.

Organize a listing

Group photos and choose their order.

Assign photos to rooms

Open a photo folder, select photos, and press G to choose a room or create one. You can also drag photos onto rooms in the sidebar.

Arrange rooms and photos

Reorder the rooms, then arrange the photos within each room. The filename previews update to reflect that sequence.

Choose what to include

Exclude photos from a job to leave them out of the numbering and processing. The original files stay on disk, and you can include them again later.

Command bar

Find actions with ⌘K.

Search for a command, room, naming preset, template, or recent job. Use the arrow keys to select a result and Return to run it.

For example, choose Add to Group to assign selected photos, or Change Group Name to rename their room. Renaming a group updates its label and filename previews.

Read the command bar guide →

Presets and templates

Save naming presets and room templates.

A naming preset defines what goes into each filename, such as the listing address, room number, room name, and photo number.

A room template saves an ordered set of rooms to apply to another listing. Edit the groups for each property without rebuilding your usual order.

Read about naming presets →

Process Photos

Review filenames before processing photos.

Choose Process X Photos at the bottom of the inspector to review all included photos in the job, regardless of the selected room or photos.

Check each filename and destination, then choose Rename only, Copy, or Move. Copy keeps your source files, Move relocates them, and Rename only changes names in their current folders.

For Copy or Move, choose a single output folder or numbered folders for each room. Processing preserves the JPG image data without re-encoding it.

Sort processed files by name before selecting them for upload. Check the final photo order in the MLS or website after uploading.

Read about processing options →

Share naming settings

Export your tags, naming presets, and room templates to a settings file. Another person can import that file to use the same setup. Photos and job history are not included.
