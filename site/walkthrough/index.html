BEFORE YOU START

Common questions.

Is this for photographers or real estate agents?

Both. Photographers can deliver an organized, consistently named set. Agents can arrange and rename the JPGs they receive before uploading them to the MLS. Both can add final details such as crops, labels, and property outlines.

Does Walkthrough change my original photos?

Outlines and crops create edited JPGs and keep the source file. Rename only changes filenames, Copy leaves source files in place, and Move relocates files. Those three processing actions preserve the JPG image data.

What can I open, and what Mac do I need?

Walkthrough works with JPG and JPEG files on macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. No account is required.

Does it publish to the MLS or find property lines?

Walkthrough prepares the files; you upload them to your MLS or website. Property outlines are drawn by you. It does not look up or verify parcel boundaries.

Can I reuse my setup or share it with someone?

Save naming presets and room templates for later jobs. Export settings to a file another person can import. Saved jobs stay on your Mac; settings sharing does not sync photos or job history.
