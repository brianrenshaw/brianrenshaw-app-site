Privacy Policy for Robot Health

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Robot Health is an iPhone app that reads Apple Health. We do not collect, sell, or share your data, and we never receive your health data. There is no developer-operated server, account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

What the app reads

Only the Apple Health data you allow on Apple’s permission screen, such as steps, calories, sleep, weight, and workouts. Robot Health never writes to Apple Health. Change access any time in the Health app under Sharing, Apps, Robot Health.

What stays on your iPhone

Your report, goals, streaks, settings, and list of recent syncs are kept on your device. We cannot see any of it.

Where your data goes

Nothing leaves your iPhone until you add a destination. Then Robot Health sends the kinds of data you chose to that place only:

  • A website or webhook whose address you paste in. Data goes directly from your iPhone over HTTPS. Its key and any extra headers are stored in your device Keychain.
  • A folder you choose in the Files app, such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox.
  • Files and pictures you share from the share sheet, to wherever you send them.

Those places are run by you or by services you chose, under their own privacy policies.

Notifications and widget

The optional notice when syncing stops is scheduled on your device. The widget shows only when the last sync happened, never a health number.

What we never do

We never use your health data for advertising or marketing, sell it, share it with anyone, or store it on our servers or in iCloud.

Data retention and deletion

Remove a destination or delete the app to remove its data from your device. Data already sent to your own destinations is yours to keep or delete there.

Children

Robot Health does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If Robot Health’s data practices change, this page and its “Last updated” date will be revised.
