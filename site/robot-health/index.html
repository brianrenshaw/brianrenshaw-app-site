Features

What it does.

Goals for anything

At least, at most, close to, or get to. Each goal keeps a streak.

Your report, your numbers

Choose from over 200 kinds of data, or start from a ready-made set. Share a picture of your day.

Everything else, grouped

Activity, Body and Food, with averages and how they compare.

Syncs on its own

In the background, from Shortcuts, or when you tap Sync now. A widget shows the last sync.

Built for your own site

Copy one prompt into the AI that built your site, then paste back the connection it gives you.

Steps at a glance

Each day’s count, runs at your goal, and today against your usual pace.

Send it anywhere

Three ways out.

  • Your website: JSON to an address you control, with a key kept in the keychain.
  • A webhook: Zapier, Make or your own script.
  • A folder: a JSON or CSV file per day in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or on your iPhone.

Private

Nothing comes to us.

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your data goes only to places you set up. Read the privacy policy.
