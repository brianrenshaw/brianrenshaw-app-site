Robot Health support

Questions or problems? Email contact@brianrenshaw.app with what you were trying to do.

Join the beta

Install Apple’s TestFlight app, then open the Robot Health beta invitation and tap Accept. Needs iOS 18 or later.

Numbers are missing

Open the Health app, then Sharing, Apps, Robot Health, and turn on what you want to see. Apple Health can’t be read while your iPhone is locked.

Set a goal

On Today, tap Goals. Pick a kind for any number and type the target. Goals show on the Day view.

Sync to your own website

In Sync, tap Add a destination, then A website. Choose your data, tap Copy the prompt, and give it to the AI that built your site. Paste back the connection it gives you. Test connection checks it without sending real data.

Sync to a webhook

Paste the webhook’s https address in the same place. Add extra headers on its page if the service needs them.

Write files to a folder

In Sync, tap Add a destination, then A folder. Pick JSON or CSV, and pounds and miles or metric. One file is written per day.

Syncing in the background

iOS decides when background syncs run. Keep Background App Refresh on and Low Power Mode off. For a set time, add Sync Health Data to a Shortcuts automation. Turn on Tell me if syncing stops in the Sync menu to hear about gaps.
