A choice for your whole group

Three ways to get the game going.

Hold a finger, take turns tapping, or flick the ball. Choose a mode from the menu to fit your group.

01 · Fingers down

Chooser

Two to five people hold a finger on the screen. After a short buildup, one ring marks who goes first.

02 · Take your turn

Tap In

Each person taps once to join. When everyone’s in, pick from the group. Room for up to 50 players.

03 · Give it a flick

Pinball

Two to twelve people tap near their seats. Flick the ball and follow the bounces to your first player. Every seat has an equal chance.

Fourteen worlds. A different feel for every game night.

Birds and watercolor, a lantern-lit tree, stained glass, dragons, or an ocean that fades into the deep. Each world brings its own illustrated board, player pieces, and Pinball details.

Tap the palette button to open Worlds. Pick a board and you’re straight back to your game. The same world carries across all three modes.

For a surprise, tap with three fingers when a choice isn’t in progress. A different world appears without clearing your group. VoiceOver offers the same option through Shuffle colors.

Less setup. Another round.

Keep your group

After a Tap In or Pinball result, tap the background to choose again with the same players. In Tap In, Undo removes an accidental extra entry.

Start in your favorite mode

Make the current mode your launch default in the mode menu. Or use Open Chooser, Open Tap In, and Open Pinball in Shortcuts to jump straight into a session.

Make yourself at home

A short welcome introduces all three modes. Replay it in Settings, or open How It Works whenever you need a reminder.
