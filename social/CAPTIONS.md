# Instagram captions and links

Draft captions for nine projects. Ingest is not included in this set yet.
Review these against the current app before posting. Copy follows the editorial rules in `MESSAGING.md`: no em dashes,
short sentences, plain language, main purpose before supporting features.

Images are built by `python3 scripts/build_instagram.py` into `social/instagram/`,
one 1080x1920 file per post. Each image already carries the icon, name, tagline,
availability and site URL, so the captions below fill in the detail. Trim them
to taste.

Instagram captions are not clickable. The URLs at the bottom are for your
profile link, a story sticker, or wherever else you want to point people.

Order follows the homepage: the iOS apps, the Mac app, the blogs, then the utility.

## 2. Folio

Image: `folio.png`

> Your HTML reports and dashboards, available offline.
>
> Save an HTML report from work or an AI chat, and Folio keeps it on your iPhone or iPad. Open it later, even with no connection.
>
> Import a page with its images, scripts, and data, so the interactive parts still work. Add a widget and choose which page it opens. Require Face ID on a page you want kept private. Turn on iCloud only if you want the same library on both devices.
>
> Saved pages work offline. Live online services still need a connection.
>
> No account required. No ads. Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

`#indieapp #iosapp #offlinefirst #productivity #appstore #indiedev`

## 3. Who's First?

Image: `whos-first.png`

> Choose who goes first. Get on with the game.
>
> Two to five people hold a finger on the screen. After a short buildup, one ring marks who goes first.
>
> Bigger group? Tap In has room for up to 50 players. Or use Pinball, where two to twelve people tap near their seats and a flicked ball finds the winner. Every seat has an equal chance.
>
> Eight visual worlds to pick from. Works offline, with support for VoiceOver, larger text, and reduced motion.
>
> Free, with no subscription, accounts, or ads. For iPhone and iPad, iOS 26 or later.

`#boardgames #gamenight #indieapp #iosapp #tabletop #appstore #familygames`

## 4. Reading Habit

Image: `reading-habit.png`

> Make reading a daily habit.
>
> Mark the days you read. No page counts, no timers, no minutes to log. However much you read, the day counts.
>
> See the days add up in your journal. Seven consecutive reading days earn one rest day, used automatically when you miss one. Choose your own monthly book goal and watch each finished book count toward it.
>
> Save recommendations with the friends who suggested them, so you know what to read next.
>
> No app account. No subscriptions. No notifications. Your library syncs through your private iCloud database, and you can export a JSON or CSV backup of your own.
>
> Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, iOS 27 or later.

`#bookstagram #readinghabit #booktracker #amreading #indieapp #iosapp #readingchallenge`

## 5. Where Do We Eat

Image: `where-do-we-eat.png`

> Make deciding where to eat easier.
>
> Save your favorites and the places you have been meaning to try. When it is time to decide, say what sounds good, pick the occasion, and add who is coming.
>
> A friend has a place they would rather skip? Add it to their do-not-recommend list. It stays out of the suggestions whenever they are joining.
>
> Get three choices, or browse every match. Afterward, keep the meal: the company, the occasion, and what you would order again.
>
> Your collection lives on your device and uses your private iCloud storage. No ads or tracking.
>
> Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, iOS 27 or later.
>
> Screens show sample data.

`#foodie #wheretoeat #datenight #restaurants #indieapp #iosapp #appstore`

## 6. Canceled

Image: `canceled.png`

> Nobody has to be the one who cancels.
>
> You and a friend each privately say whether you want out. If you both do, the plan is off. If not, nobody finds out you wavered.
>
> The plan is still on unless you both cancel.
>
> Only the person who made the plan needs the app. The friend can accept or cancel in a browser. Calendar import is opt-in and starts with nothing selected.
>
> In development. No download yet.
>
> Screens show example data from the app's Demo mode.

`#indieapp #iosapp #indiedev #buildinpublic #comingsoon`

## 7. Walkthrough

Image: `walkthrough.png`

> Organize photos for a property listing.
>
> Group photos by room, set their order, and export copies with numbered filenames. Save room templates and naming presets for future listings.
>
> Press ⌘K to find commands for assigning rooms, renaming groups, applying presets, and reopening jobs.
>
> Review filenames and destinations before choosing Rename only, Copy, or Move. Copy keeps the source files. Export preserves the JPG image data without re-encoding it.
>
> Sort exported files by name before selecting them for upload, then check the final order in the MLS or website.
>
> For macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. No account required.
>
> Screens show repeated demonstration photos.

`#realestate #realtor #listingphotos #realestatephotography #mls #macapp #realestatemarketing`

## Claims to keep straight

- **Folio** has no stated minimum iOS version anywhere in this repo. Do not invent one.
- **Reading Habit** has no Mac release. The Mac build is in development. Do not
  claim a Mac download, a macOS minimum, or an Apple silicon requirement.
- **Who's First?** requires iOS 26. The other three released iOS apps require
  iOS 27. They are not interchangeable in a spec line.
- **Canceled** is in development. There is no download, no beta link, and no
  call to action. Notifications are off in the beta service, so do not promise
  reminders or push. `canceledapp.com` is not the app yet, so do not link it.
  Spelling is Canceled, one L. Its screens come from Demo mode and are always
  captioned as example data.
- **Walkthrough** has no sync service. Say "share your setup", never "sync".
- **Where Do We Eat** screenshots use fictional restaurants and people. Caption
  them as sample data.
- The TestFlight beta for Where Do We Eat is over. Never link `testflight.apple.com`.
- "No ads, no tracking" is verified from the privacy policies of the five apps.
  Do not extend that phrasing to the blogs or the browser tools, which have no
  such policy in this repo. Lankford Legends' own card says "ad-free", which is
  the claim to reuse for it.
